## Overview

The **WOLFSBURG AWARD for Urban Vision** is a biennial open ideas competition hosted by the City of Wolfsburg. It invites European university students to submit innovative concepts across architecture, neighbourhood planning, mobility design, and related fields.

---

## Eligibility

Students enrolled at European universities, colleges, or universities of applied sciences in:
- Architecture
- Interior design
- Landscape architecture
- Urban planning
- Monument preservation
- Fine arts
- Related disciplines

Both individual students and university courses may participate. University courses are registered by their supervisor.

---

## Theme

> "No specifications for the theme or design area. Everything is welcome."

The competition seeks contributions that examine Wolfsburg's architectural quality and unique history. Experimental approaches are encouraged.

---

## Prize

Total prize pool: **5,000 EUR** distributed among winners.

## Registration and Submission

- Registration via online form
- All entries submitted digitally to a designated submission folder
- Upload link + access data sent to the registered email address ~3 weeks before the deadline
- **Format:** max. **2× DIN A1** or **1× DIN A0** sheets; textual explanations placed on the sheets themselves
- **File types:** printable **PDF** (original size) + printable **TIFF** (original size) + printable **JPG** (A4)

## Rules & Anonymity — IMPORTANT

> **Participants are obliged to maintain the public anonymity of their work until the award ceremony.**
> The submitted work may carry **no indication of the identity of the author(s)**, and the entry (including the physical model) must be identified in all parts only by a **self-selected six-digit code number**.

**What this means for us (Parking City → Hub City):**

- The **award ceremony is November 2026** — so the anonymity obligation runs *well past* the Aug 16 submission deadline, not just up to it.
- **No attributed public posting of THIS project before Nov 2026** — that includes **LinkedIn / social media with our names or Bauhaus-Universität Weimar attached**, and **credited public exhibition** of the entry.
- ⚠️ **Open conflict:** the **Summaery Exhibition (July 9–12, 2026)** is a public, attributed university show *before* judging. Studio exhibitions are common and sometimes tolerated, but this is a disqualification-level rule — **confirm in writing with the organizer** (Esther Orant, wolfsburgaward@stadt.wolfsburg.de) before treating it as OK.
- The safe line: do not publicly link **this project** to **our identities** until after the award ceremony.
