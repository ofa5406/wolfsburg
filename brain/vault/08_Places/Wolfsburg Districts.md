# Wolfsburg Districts

The spatial units of every analysis in the project: **44 districts** (Stadtteile), with boundaries held as pre-processed GeoJSON in the Activity Map and scored by each of its modes — connectivity per mode, green coverage, social density, encounter potential ([[Wolfsburg Activity Map]], [[Social Vitality Scoring]]).

The tool defines the **city centre as nine central districts**: Stadtmitte, Schillerteich, Rothenfelde, Wohltberg, Volkswagenwerk, Alt-Wolfsburg, Hellwinkel, Heßlingen and Hohenstein — the destination set against which every district's connectivity is measured ([[Mobility Mode]]), and approximately the study area whose 17,400 residents anchor the demand basis ([[Demand Basis]]).

Reading the scores across the 44 is where the project's siting logic comes from: districts scoring high on automobile connectivity but low on cycling mark the mobility gap ([[Rad Network]]); low encounter-potential districts are where reclaimed parking buys the most social return ([[Reclaimed Land]]); and the hub algorithm spreads candidates across them with its density filter ([[Intermodal Hub Algorithm]], [[Stadtmitte]]).
