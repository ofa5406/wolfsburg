# Wolfsburg Hauptbahnhof

The city's rail gateway and the system's fixed point: Wolfsburg's main station sits on the ICE line between Berlin and Hannover, directly across the canal from the plant and adjacent to Autostadt — the one interchange whose location no algorithm gets to choose ([[Wolfsburg]], [[Autostadt]]).

In the network design the Hauptbahnhof is an Order-1 destination of the cycling algorithm ([[Rad Network Algorithm]]) and the natural senior partner of the hub system: regional rail delivers a large share of the ~18,000 in-commuters, and the station forecourt is where they transfer to the fleet — the L-tier interchange condition at its purest ([[Intermodal Transfer]], [[L-Hub]]).

The station also names the system's one hard external dependency: rail timetables and ticketing belong to DB and the region, not the city — which is why the integrated-ticket mandate is booked as a policy precondition rather than assumed ([[Integrated Ticketing]], [[MaaS Is Governance-Hard]]). The [[Park-and-Switch Interchange]] logic wraps the station's park-and-ride role into the L-Gateway edge ([[L-Gateway]]).
