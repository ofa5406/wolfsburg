# VW Factory Gate

The project's primary case-study site ([[VW Gate Case Study Decision]]): the hub adjacent to the Volkswagen factory campus, where the system meets its hardest condition — the shift wave's ~10,000 workers through one interchange in about an hour ([[VW Shift Wave]]).

The gate hub is designed as an L-tier anchor on the trunk network: autonomous buses and pooled shuttles surge here in pre-positioned waves ([[Autonomous Bus]], [[Pre-positioning]]), the [[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]] organises the crowd from vehicle edge to dwelling edge, and the [[Capacity Analysis]] table is computed for this exact location. Anna's proof journey terminates here; Thomas boards here every morning ([[Anna]], [[Thomas]]).

The site's symbolic load equals its operational one: the gate is where the parking city reproduced itself daily — thousands of private cars arriving to build more private cars — and the design remakes it as the place where the shared system boards ([[Post-Car City in VW's Hometown]]). Cause and cure, one address ([[Worlds Largest Car Factory]], [[Commuter Corridor]]).
