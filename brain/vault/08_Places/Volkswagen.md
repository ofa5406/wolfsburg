# Volkswagen

The institution the city orbits. Volkswagen is Wolfsburg's founder, largest employer (~60,000 at the plant, roughly the whole working population tied to the group or its partners), landowner, identity-maker — and, decisively for the project, **the manufacturer of the post-car system's own components** ([[Worlds Largest Car Factory]], [[Wolfsburg Privileged Identity Study]]).

The company appears in the brain in three roles. As **context**: its restructuring — up to 50,000 German job cuts, Golf production leaving for Mexico in 2027 — is the disruption window the behaviour-change strategy launches into ([[VW Cuts 35000 Jobs]], [[Golf Moves to Puebla]]). As **strategy**: the NEW AUTO pivot from car-seller to mobility-services provider is the narrative that turns the project from attack into demonstration ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]]). As **supplier and operator**: MOIA's ride-pooling operation, the production Level-4 ID. Buzz AD, and the turnkey licensing model are the fleet's industrial base ([[MOIA]], [[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]).

The project's stance is fixed: VW enters as co-author, never adversary — the shift wave is its gift to the system, and the system is its showroom ([[VW Shift Wave]], [[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]).
