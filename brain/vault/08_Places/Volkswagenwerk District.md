# Volkswagenwerk District

The district that *is* the factory: Volkswagenwerk, on the north bank of the Mittellandkanal, contains the plant's 1.6 km² of halls and 75 km of internal roads — one of the nine central districts by the Activity Map's definition, and the strangest of them: a city-centre district whose population is a workforce ([[Worlds Largest Car Factory]], [[Wolfsburg Districts]]).

Its analytical profile is the project's demand story in miniature: extreme connectivity scores on every mode (everything leads to the plant), near-zero residential catchment, and an activity rhythm that pulses with the shift clock rather than the shopping day ([[Mobility Mode]], [[VW Shift Wave]]).

Design-wise it is served rather than transformed: the [[VW Factory Gate]] hub sits at its threshold, the trunk network terminates on it, and the canal crossings that bind it to the residential south bank are the corridors the fleet is pre-positioned around ([[Commuter Corridor]], [[Mittellandkanal]]). The district is the fixed demand pole the whole network is calibrated against ([[Demand Basis]]).
