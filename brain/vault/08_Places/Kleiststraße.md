# Kleiststraße

The named street of the street-transformation deliverable: **Kleiststraße is the first of the before/after section pairs** the proof framework requires — the ordinary Wolfsburg street chosen to show what the transformation means at the scale of a front door ([[Street Transformation Sections]], [[Proof Framework]]).

The choice of an unremarkable street is the argument. Flagship sites transform in every competition entry; the thesis stands or falls on whether the *typical* street — carriageway, parked cars both sides, narrow pavements — gains anything real. The before section documents the existing allocation; the after section spends the reclaimed kerb space on wider footways, protected cycling on the [[Rad Network]], trees, seating and an S-hub footprint in a former parking bay ([[S-Hub]], [[On-Street Parking 20 Percent of Kerb Space]]).

Dated to the implementation phases, the pair reads as a construction sequence rather than a vision board ([[Implementation Timeline]], [[Street-Space Reallocation]]) — and it gives every juror and visitor the same test to apply to their own street.
