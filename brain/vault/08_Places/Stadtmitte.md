# Stadtmitte

The centre of the centre: Stadtmitte is Wolfsburg's core district — Porschestraße's pedestrian spine, the commercial heart, the town hall — and the first of the nine central districts the Activity Map treats as the city's destination set ([[Wolfsburg Districts]]).

In the analyses Stadtmitte is the gravitational reference: every district's connectivity score counts routes that reach it ([[Mobility Mode]]), the facilities mode shows its venue cluster brightening through the day ([[Facilities Mode]]), and the hub algorithm's density filter exists precisely because raw scoring would pile every hub here ([[Intermodal Hub Algorithm]]).

In the design it is the district where the five-zone structure bites hardest — through-traffic filtered off the core grid ([[Five-Zone Model]]) — and where the land dividend is most visible per square metre: central surface parking is the most valuable asphalt in the city, and its conversion to plazas, planting and M-hub surfaces is the transformation's shop window ([[Reclaimed Land]], [[M-Hub]], [[Public-Space Dividend]]).
