# Mittellandkanal

The water line through the project. The Mittellandkanal — Germany's longest artificial waterway — runs east–west through Wolfsburg and separates the VW plant on its north bank from the city centre on the south: the geography that makes the factory commute a *crossing* and the canal edge the city's most charged seam ([[Wolfsburg]], [[Worlds Largest Car Factory]]).

In the project the canal appears twice. As **movement space**: the crossing points concentrate the shift wave into countable corridors — the geometry the fleet's trunk modes are pre-positioned around ([[VW Shift Wave]], [[Commuter Corridor]]). As **reclaimable edge**: stretches of canal-side land currently serve as surface parking, and their conversion to waterfront park is one of the project's named land-dividend proposals — the Activity Map's greenery network layer exists partly to draw this before/after ([[Reclaimed Land]], [[Wolfsburg Activity Map]]).

A city that stores cars on its waterfront is the parking city summarised in one image; the canal park is the counter-image ([[Public-Space Dividend]], [[Car-Dedicated Land]]).
