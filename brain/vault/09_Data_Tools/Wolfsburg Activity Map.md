# Wolfsburg Activity Map

The project's analytical instrument: an interactive browser map of Wolfsburg, built by the team, that pulls live geography from OpenStreetMap, runs the project's algorithms over it, and renders the results as switchable analysis modes ([[OpenStreetMap Data]]). Navigation is organised into five sections — Strategy, Capacity, Hub System, Urban Design, Simulation — over the underlying modes: [[Mobility Mode]], [[Facilities Mode]], [[Greenery Mode]], [[Social Vitality Scoring]], [[Intermodal Hub Algorithm]], [[Rad Network Algorithm]], Earth (satellite), and the [[Fleet Calculation Dashboard]].

Its role is stated in the workshop briefing: **a design and analysis instrument, not the final deliverable** — the place where siting logic, coverage claims and fleet numbers are computed and stress-tested before they appear on boards ([[Proof Framework]]). Its components are embedded live in the exhibition deck rather than screenshotted ([[Exhibition Deck]]).

The brain tracks its honest edges alongside its outputs: the tool's fleet total differs from the locked decision ([[Fleet Number Conflict]]), its service radii are not walking catchments ([[Coverage Radius Mismatch]]), and its methodology page outruns its code ([[Methodology vs Code Gap]]). MOIA's MIA is its professional benchmark ([[MIA Mobility Impact Analyzer]]).
