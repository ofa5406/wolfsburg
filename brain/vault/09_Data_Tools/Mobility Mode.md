# Mobility Mode

The Activity Map's connectivity analysis: all 44 districts coloured by **how well each connects to the city centre**, per mode — public transport, automobile, cycling, walking — with the centre defined as the nine central districts ([[Wolfsburg Districts]]).

The method is route-counting with a strict rule: a route counts for a district only if at least one point lies inside the district *and* the same route reaches the centre — passing through without arriving earns nothing. Raw counts rescale to 1–10 ([[OpenStreetMap Data]]).

The four sub-layers read together are the diagnosis. Automobile scores are near-uniformly high — the baseline that *demonstrates* car dominance rather than asserting it ([[Car Dependency]]). Cycling and transit scores expose the gap district by district: a neighbourhood scoring 8 on automobile and 2 on cycling is a priority zone for the [[Rad Network]], and the systematic shortfall is the mobility-injustice map the project's interventions answer ([[Modal Split]], [[Multimodal Network]]).
