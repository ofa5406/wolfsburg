# ID Buzz AD Production Level 4

The project's strongest "this is not science fiction" card: Volkswagen's MOIA has launched the **series-production Level-4 ID. Buzz AD** — a ride-pooling minibus with 27 sensors (13 cameras, 9 LiDAR, 5 radar) — deploying **more than 500 vehicles in Hamburg during 2026**, initially with safety drivers, plus a partnership with Uber targeting several thousand vehicles in US cities over the coming decade, with Los Angeles testing under way.

Every element aligns with the Hub City's fleet design: a *pooling* vehicle, not a single-party robotaxi; *production*, not prototype; introduced *supervised* and phased driverless — the exact autonomy strategy the project assumes ([[Phased Autonomy]], [[Shuttle Pod]]).

And it is made by the company headquartered at the end of the project's commuter corridor. Wolfsburg proposing shared autonomous pooling is not importing a foreign fantasy; it is asking VW's hometown to run the vehicle VW already builds ([[Volkswagen]], [[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]). Hamburg-to-other-German-cities is the natural diffusion path, and Wolfsburg sits on it.
