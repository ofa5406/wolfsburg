# Waymo 450000 Weekly Rides

The existence proof for road-speed urban autonomy: by December 2025 Waymo was delivering **~450,000 paid driverless rides per week** — up from ~175,000 at the start of the year — with roughly **2,500 vehicles** across Phoenix, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Austin, Atlanta and Miami, freeway driving included.

Two implications carry into the project. First, the technology argument is over: full-speed driverless operation in mixed city traffic is a commercial service, not a demo ([[Robotaxi Model]]). Second, the *shape* of the success is instructive — geographically gated, expanding city by city, a decade and billions of dollars deep. Autonomy scales like infrastructure, not like software ([[Phased Autonomy]]).

The efficiency footnote matters most for Wolfsburg: 450,000 weekly rides from 2,500 vehicles is ~26 trips per vehicle per day, single-party rides. The Hub City's fleet arithmetic asks less of its large vehicles and multiplies throughput by pooling — the demand it must serve is denser and more predictable than anything Waymo faces ([[Trips per Vehicle per Day]]).
