# Worlds Largest Car Factory

The scale of the anchor institution: the Wolfsburg plant employs **~60,000 people**, making it the world's largest single car factory — halls covering **1.6 km²** threaded by 75 km of internal roads, in a city of ~130,000 where roughly the entire working population is tied to the company or its partners ([[Volkswagen]]).

For the mobility system, the plant is a demand generator without European parallel: ~18,000 workers commute in from outside while shift waves of ~10,000 move through the gates within an hour ([[VW Shift Wave]], [[Demand Basis]]). One origin-destination pair carries a load most cities spread across a whole centre — the concentrated, predictable geometry that pooled autonomous transport serves best ([[Commuter Corridor]]).

For the urban thesis, the plant explains the pathology it must cure: a city built in service of one factory optimised itself for the factory's product, and the result is Germany's most car-saturated urban fabric ([[Wolfsburg Highest Car Density]], [[Parking City]]). The [[VW Factory Gate]] hub is where cause and cure meet.
