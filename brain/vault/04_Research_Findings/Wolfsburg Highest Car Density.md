# Wolfsburg Highest Car Density

The project's hero statistic: **956 passenger cars per 1,000 residents** (January 2025) — the **highest car density in all of Germany**, against a national average of 590 and Berlin's 334. Nearly one car per man, woman and child; more cars than driving-age residents.

Every strand of the argument routes through this number. It sizes the problem: at dwelling-scale land per car ([[61 Square Metres per Parking Space]]), Wolfsburg's ~120,000 cars hoard a district's worth of city. It selects the site: nowhere is the parking city more purely expressed, so nowhere is the counter-model more meaningful ([[Parking City]], [[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]). And it arms the rhetoric: *if a post-car city can work here, it can work anywhere* ([[Post-Car City in VW's Hometown]]).

The figure also quietly defines success: the gap between 956 and Berlin's 334 is the transformation's full range, and every hub that opens moves the number ([[From Parking City to Hub City]]).
