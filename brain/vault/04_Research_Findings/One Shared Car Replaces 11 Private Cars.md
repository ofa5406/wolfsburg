# One Shared Car Replaces 11 Private Cars

The German national benchmark: the Bundesverband CarSharing (bcs) reports that each **station-based shared car replaces about 11 private cars**; free-floating estimates scatter from 8 up to 23, and the European Transport Safety Council rounds to ~12. The peer-reviewed literature adds the crucial asymmetry — the effect is robust for station-based sharing and weak-to-insignificant for free-floating ([[Free-Floating Substitution Evidence Is Weak]]).

The finding does two jobs in the brain. It corroborates the substitution corridor from simulation and from Bremen's field data ([[One-for-Ten Substitution]], [[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]]) — three independent methods landing on the same order of magnitude is what makes the ratio load-bearing. And it validates a specific design decision: the Hub City's shared cars are station-based at hubs, the variant the evidence actually supports ([[Station-Based Car-Sharing]], [[Shared EV Car]]).

Quote it as "the German carsharing association's own evaluation," and note it is survey-derived.
