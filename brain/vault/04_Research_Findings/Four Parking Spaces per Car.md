# Four Parking Spaces per Car

In car-dependent regions, every car is served by **up to four parking spaces** — one at home, one at work, and fractional shares at every shop, clinic and destination it might visit, all mandated into existence by parking minimums. Los Angeles County holds ~3.3 spaces per car; parking covers an estimated **14% of its incorporated land**, and studies put parking above 5% of all urban land, rising to a quarter or more of floor area in dense centres.

The ratio explains why removing *cars* releases several times more *land* than the cars themselves occupy: the parking network is built for the car's whole itinerary, not its resting position. When a shared vehicle replaces many private ones ([[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]]), the multiplier works in reverse — one hub bay retires a whole constellation of mandated spaces.

These are US figures; the brain flags them as direction-not-magnitude for Wolfsburg, whose own stock is measured in the [[Car-Land Composite Map]].
