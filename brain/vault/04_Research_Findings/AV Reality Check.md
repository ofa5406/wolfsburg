# AV Reality Check

The project's honest audit of autonomous mobility, mid-2026. Four data points define the terrain. **Waymo works**: ~450,000 paid driverless rides a week with ~2,500 vehicles — but geographically gated, a decade and billions in the making ([[Waymo 450000 Weekly Rides]]). **Cruise failed**: shut down after one safety incident, a regulatory crisis and >$10B in losses — autonomy is not a one-way ratchet ([[Cruise Shutdown]]). **The slow campus shuttle stalled**: EasyMile and Navya's 15–25 km/h class logged hundreds of pilots that largely never converted to fleets ([[Low-Speed Shuttle Class]]). **VW ships the relevant vehicle**: MOIA's production Level-4 ID. Buzz AD launches in Hamburg in 2026 ([[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]).

The synthesis the project builds on: autonomy arrives **in waves, on the easiest geometries first** — predictable corridors, supervised operation, geofenced zones — not as a switch that flips ([[Phased Autonomy]]). Industry timelines slip ~1–2 years per survey cycle ([[AV Timelines Keep Slipping]]); the design must survive the slippage, and the [[VW Shift Wave]] corridor is precisely the easy geometry that arrives first.
