# 25 Percent Traffic Cut Saves 200 Lives

The finding that protects the project from utopian framing: ISGlobal modelled a scenario in which Barcelona cuts traffic by just **25%** — and still found **~200 premature deaths prevented per year** and a **17.6% drop in NO₂**. The health dividend is not gated behind a total transformation; it scales from the first quarter of the curve ([[Barcelona Health Dividend]]).

Strategically this is the project's insurance policy. If autonomy slips, if politics slows the rollout, if only the first phase of the [[Implementation Timeline]] lands — the intervention already pays in lives. Partial success is genuine success, which is precisely what makes the phased, reversible strategy credible rather than all-or-nothing ([[Tactical Urbanism]]).

It also sets an honest benchmark for Wolfsburg: the hub network needs to shift a quarter of car traffic, not eliminate it, before the health argument becomes measurable in local mortality statistics ([[Modal Shift]]).
