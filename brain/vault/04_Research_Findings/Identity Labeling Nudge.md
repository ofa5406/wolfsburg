# Identity Labeling Nudge

The soft counterpart to hard defaults: a Rotterdam commuter experiment found that **social-identity labelling — telling people "you're a sustainable traveler" — measurably increases transit use**, by letting them claim a valued identity rather than obey an instruction. People act to become who they are told they already are.

The finding matters in Wolfsburg because identity is the battleground: car identity predicts resistance to car reduction ([[Car Identity Predicts Resistance]]), so the transformation needs an equally wearable identity on its side. The project's offer — pioneer of VW's own future, resident of the world's reference post-car city ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]], [[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]) — is the identity-label nudge scaled to a whole town: pride in place transferred to the new system rather than confiscated with the old one.

At the service level the applications are small and cheap: membership framing in the [[Mobility as a Service]] app, hub signage that addresses users as members of the system, early-adopter recognition in the pilot zones ([[Behaviour Change]]). Identity is infrastructure too.
