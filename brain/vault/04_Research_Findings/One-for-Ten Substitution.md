# One-for-Ten Substitution

The ITF's headline ratio: at 100% shared autonomous mobility, **one shared vehicle substitutes for about ten private cars** (two-for-ten at 50% adoption). The figure sits between the German car-sharing evidence — eleven private cars replaced per station-based shared car ([[One Shared Car Replaces 11 Private Cars]]) — and Bremen's measured sixteen ([[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]]), giving the project a three-source corridor of 10–16:1 from simulation, national statistics and a peer city's own impact study.

Applied to Wolfsburg, the corridor sizes the transformation: 763 shared vehicles stand in for roughly **8,000–12,000 private cars** by the most conservative reading — and the city's stock is ~120,000 ([[Wolfsburg Highest Car Density]]), which is why the project pairs fleet substitution with modal shift to walking, cycling and pooled trunk modes rather than claiming the fleet alone dissolves the parking city ([[Modal Distribution Model]]).

Honest usage: quote the range, name the sources, and let the conservatism of 763 speak for itself ([[Fleet Sizing]]).
