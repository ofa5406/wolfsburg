# Golf Moves to Puebla

The specific disruption with a date: **Golf production leaves Wolfsburg for Puebla, Mexico, from 2027**. The plant that defined the city consolidates from four assembly lines to two and pivots to EVs (ID.3, Cupra Born) — the deepest reorganisation of Wolfsburg's daily rhythm since the plant was built.

For the project this is the **calendar anchor of the behaviour-change strategy**. Shift patterns, line assignments and thousands of commute routines will be rewritten in 2027 regardless of what the city does; travel habits will be broken open by force ([[Forced Disruption as Opportunity]]). The only question is what is standing at the kerb when they are: if the first hubs, the shift shuttles and the pre-loaded mobility offer are live *during* the reorganisation, the new commutes form inside the new system ([[Moments of Change]], [[Implementation Timeline]]).

The symbolism is not lost on the brain: the car that built the parking city leaves, and the space it vacates — on the line and on the street — is where the next system takes shape ([[From Parking City to Hub City]]).
