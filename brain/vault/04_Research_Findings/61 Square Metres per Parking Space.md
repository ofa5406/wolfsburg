# 61 Square Metres per Parking Space

[[Donald Shoup]]'s land arithmetic: a parking space consumes roughly **660 square feet — about 61 m²** — once aisles and circulation are counted. That is enough floor area for a small apartment, and about a quarter of a typical single-family house. Narrower European measures put the bay-plus-circulation figure at 25–40 m²; either way, the order of magnitude is the point — *car storage costs dwelling-sized land, per car, per location*.

This is the multiplier that converts the project's thesis into quantity. Every space dissolved into the hub network releases a bedroom-to-studio-sized patch of city, and Wolfsburg — with the highest car density in Germany ([[Wolfsburg Highest Car Density]]) — holds one of the country's largest reserves of exactly this land ([[Car-Dedicated Land]]).

The figure is US-derived; the brain records it as a mechanism-scale number, not a German survey value. The [[Car-Land Composite Map]] measures Wolfsburg's own stock.
