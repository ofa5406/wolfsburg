# LTN Active Travel Evidence

The outcome data from the largest natural experiment in filtered permeability: London implemented ~100 Low-Traffic Neighbourhood schemes by 2022, and the Aldred & Goodman *People and Places* survey found residents inside LTNs showed **lower car use and more active travel**, with displacement onto boundary roads far smaller and more temporary than critics predicted ([[Disappearing Traffic Study]]).

This is the empirical layer under the project's zone structure: [[Filtered Permeability]] supplies the principle, the [[Modal Filter]] the device, and the LTN literature the measured behavioural result — the triad that makes Wolfsburg's restricted zone entrances an evidenced design, not an ideological one ([[Low-Traffic Neighbourhood]]).

The evidence carries its politics with it: LTN studies also document polarised community responses and equity concerns about who benefits. The brain stores both halves deliberately — the effect is real *and* the backlash is real ([[Imposition Without Consent]]) — because a jury will know about the controversy, and the project's answer is process, not denial.
