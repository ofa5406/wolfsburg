# NEW AUTO Strategy

The company's own words, and the project's narrative keystone: Volkswagen's **"NEW AUTO — Mobility for Generations to Come"** strategy publicly commits the group to realigning from vehicle manufacturer to **"software-driven mobility provider,"** with revenue shifting from car sales toward software and mobility services. MOIA is the operating arm — autonomous ride-pooling, AI fleet management and a MaaS platform sold as turnkey systems *to cities* ([[MOIA]], [[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]).

This inverts the project's political geometry. A post-car Wolfsburg is not a rejection of the company that built the town — it is **the live demonstrator of the company's own declared future**: the home town as the world's reference installation of what VW sells. Mobility hubs are the showroom for VW's next business, not a threat to its last one ([[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]).

The framing carries the consent strategy: VW, the Works Council and IG Metall enter as co-authors of their own pivot rather than defendants of the status quo ([[VW Cuts 35000 Jobs]], [[Wolfsburg Privileged Identity Study]]).
