# Parking Space Costs 20 to 35 Thousand

A structured parking space is a building: **$20,000–35,000 per space** above grade, $35,000–100,000 underground; a 500-space garage totals $10–15 million. The German anchor is Freiburg-Vauban, where a household buys its space in the edge garage for about **$40,000**, unbundled from the home ([[Freiburg Vauban]]).

Two arguments flow from the cost. First, the six multi-storey car parks the project converts into L-hubs represent tens of millions of euros of *already-spent* structure — [[Adaptive Reuse]] captures that sunk investment instead of demolishing it ([[L-Hub]]). Second, every space *not* built is real money: Bremen values the garage construction its car-sharing network made unnecessary at **over €100 million** ([[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]]), which is the fiscal face of the substitution effect.

The brain quotes construction figures as US costs converted loosely; Vauban supplies the clean German number.
