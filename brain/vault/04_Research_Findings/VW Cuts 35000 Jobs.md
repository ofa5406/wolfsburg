# VW Cuts 35000 Jobs

The backdrop against which the project lands: Volkswagen plans to cut **~35,000 jobs in Germany** (reports run to 50,000 group-wide), with ~10% pay cuts and freezes through 2025–26. Thousands of workers have protested at Wolfsburg headquarters; anxiety in the company town is acute and current.

The restructuring cuts both ways for the project. As threat: proposing "less car" to a town frightened of losing its car jobs is politically radioactive if it reads as one more thing being taken away ([[Wolfsburg Privileged Identity Study]]). As opening: the same restructuring is a **mass moment of change** — reorganised shifts, redeployed workers, rewritten commutes — precisely the window in which travel habits become changeable ([[Moments of Change Evidence]], [[Golf Moves to Puebla]]).

The project's framing rule follows: the Hub City must arrive as part of the town's economic future — the demonstrator of VW's mobility-services pivot ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]]) — never as the urbanists' verdict on its past. Opportunity, not loss, or nothing at all.
