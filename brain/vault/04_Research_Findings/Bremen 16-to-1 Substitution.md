# Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution

The field-measured substitution ratio from the project's key German precedent: Bremen's 2018 impact analysis (team red, surveying 1,563 users) found **each car-sharing vehicle replaces about 16 private cars**. The network's cumulative effect: 20,000+ users, **more than 6,000 private cars removed** — about 30 km of street if parked nose to tail — and street relief the city itself values as equivalent to **over €100 million** of parking-garage construction it never had to build ([[Bremen mobil.punkt]]).

Car-sharing households also drive less than half the Bremen average distance — the substitution changes behaviour, not just badge ownership.

This is the engine number of the parking-to-public-life conversion: it makes the arithmetic literal. Pool vehicles at a hub, and each one retires a street-length of parked metal; multiply across 68 hubs and the reclaimed land the thesis promises stops being rhetorical ([[Public-Space Dividend]]).

Present it as "Bremen's own study found" — a survey-based estimate from a real network, not a physical law.
