# Cruise Shutdown

The downside proof in the autonomy record: General Motors **shut down Cruise in December 2024** after a pedestrian-dragging incident, the regulatory crisis that followed, and more than **$10 billion in operating losses**. A robotaxi program with a major manufacturer behind it, live in multiple cities, ended in months — refocused into driver-assist features.

The finding disciplines the project in two ways. Technically, it proves autonomy is **not a one-way ratchet**: public trust and regulatory permission can be lost overnight, so the Hub City's system is designed to run with safety drivers and supervision for as long as needed, degrading gracefully rather than betting the city on uninterrupted progress ([[Phased Autonomy]]).

Politically, it calibrates the pitch. A jury that has read the news will probe autonomous claims with Cruise in mind; the project answers by leading with the phased design and VW's production vehicle rather than a leap of faith ([[AV Reality Check]], [[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]).
