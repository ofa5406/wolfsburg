# Risk — Company Town Politics

The identity risk: *"You're proposing a post-private-car city in the home of Volkswagen — attacking the town's economic base. Politically naïve, locally offensive."* Mishandled, the project reads as outsiders lecturing autoworkers amid mass layoffs ([[Wolfsburg Privileged Identity Study]], [[VW Cuts 35000 Jobs]]).

The counter is the project's strongest reframe, and it is factual, not rhetorical: **VW is already pivoting** from car-seller to mobility-services provider — the NEW AUTO strategy, MOIA's turnkey autonomous ridepooling product, the Uber deployment deal ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]], [[MOIA]]). And the "threat to jobs" frame is overtaken by reality: the manufacturing monoculture is contracting regardless — up to 50,000 German jobs, ~15,000 in Wolfsburg, Golf production leaving for Mexico ([[Golf Moves to Puebla]]). The status quo is not a safe harbour; the project offers the town a **second economic identity** ([[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]).

The deck's one-liner: *"We're not against VW — we're with the VW that's becoming MOIA."* The action behind it: VW, Works Council and IG Metall enter as co-authors from Phase 0 ([[Implementation Timeline]], [[Post-Car City in VW's Hometown]]).
