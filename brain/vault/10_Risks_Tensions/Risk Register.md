# Risk Register

The project's red-team document: the eight hardest objections a critical tutor or competition jury will raise, each with the objection stated bluntly, the reason it is a real risk, an evidence-backed counter, and a concrete pre-deadline action. Maintained in `research/07_weak-points-actions.md`; priority-flagged for the June 25 studio final.

The register, by severity: the three 🔴 proof-killers — autonomous vehicles aren't ready ([[Risk — AV Dependence]]), the fleet math is made up ([[Risk — Fleet Math Challenged]]), and it's just an app ([[Risk — Tech Saviour Critique]]); the 🟠 political and structural risks — company-town politics ([[Risk — Company Town Politics]]), German car culture ([[Risk — Car Culture Resistance]]), financing ([[Risk — Financing Gap]]), equity ([[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]]); and the 🟡 operational objection — freight, emergencies and displaced traffic ([[Freight and Emergency Access]]).

The register's method is the project's epistemic signature: name the weakness before the jury does, answer with cited evidence, convert what cannot be answered into a roadmap item ([[Proof Framework]], [[Methodology vs Code Gap]]).
