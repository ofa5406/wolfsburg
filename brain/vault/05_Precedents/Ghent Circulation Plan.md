# Ghent Circulation Plan

The proof that the Groningen model still works in the 2020s — cheaply and fast. In 2017 Ghent divided its centre into **six sectors** around a car-free core; crossing between sectors requires the R40 ring. The whole plan was implemented **over one weekend for about €5 million**: ~80 streets reconfigured, 2,500 signs, 14 modal filters ([[Groningen Traffic Circulation Plan]], [[Modal Filter]]).

The outcomes justify the audacity: car modal share fell **from 55% to 27%** of trips; centre cycling rose ~50–60%, hitting the city's 35% modal-share target **eleven years early**; car traffic in residential streets dropped 58%; NOx fell ~20%; tourism and street life grew. The responsible alderman was **re-elected and promoted** — delivery, then consent ([[Imposition Without Consent]]).

For the jury, Ghent is the feasibility slide: a sector plan is signage, filters and ring management, not megastructure. For the budget question, one weekend and €5 million is the answer. Six Ghent sectors ≈ Wolfsburg's five zones; their ring ≈ the restricted-entrance perimeter ([[Five-Zone Model]]).
