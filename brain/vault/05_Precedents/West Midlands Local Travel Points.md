# West Midlands Local Travel Points

The UK's first branded mobility-hub network — and the most explicit kit-of-parts precedent on record. The West Midlands authority describes its Local Travel Points as *"a collection of modular components which can be assembled to reinvent how we use parking bays and small public spaces"* — the project's design method, stated as policy ([[Kit-of-Parts]]).

The component menu is nearly the Wolfsburg toolpalette: cycle hire, secure bike hangars, e-scooters, e-cargo bikes, car club with EV charging, wayfinding maps, real-time information, tool and repair stations, seating, greenery, parcel lockers ([[Hub Toolpalette]]). Identity was built through engagement and **commissioned public art** — a Black Country artist co-made a sculpture with residents — and two Halesowen hubs earned CoMoUK **Gold** accreditation, the UK's only hub quality standard ([[Art and Cultural Marker]]).

The precise transfer: hubs assembled *from parking bays* — the S-hub's exact move ([[S-Hub]]) — from a modular catalogue, with local art carrying place identity inside a network identity. San Diego's SANDAG publishes the same idea as a features catalogue; the pattern is international ([[Hub Component Ladder]]).
