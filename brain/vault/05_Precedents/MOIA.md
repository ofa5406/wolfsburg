# MOIA

The ally precedent the whole project stands on — because it belongs to Volkswagen. MOIA is the VW Group's mobility subsidiary and **Europe's largest public-transport on-demand ridepooling service**: 11M+ rides in Hamburg (and historically Hannover), ~450 electric six-seaters, 12,500+ virtual stops (10,000 wheelchair-accessible), a 4.8-star app — now deploying the production Level-4 **ID. Buzz AD** ([[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]) and pivoting to a **B2B licensing model**: selling cities a turnkey ridepooling system — booking app, demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, fleet management, consulting ([[MIA Mobility Impact Analyzer]]).

MOIA's own mission line — autonomous ridepooling *"can make a decisive contribution to closing the gap between individual and public transport"* — is the project's thesis, written by Volkswagen. It defuses the three hardest objections at once: the tech is productised, not sci-fi; the plan is *pro-VW*, aligned with the company's stated strategy ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]]); and the operator question has a ready answer headquartered next door ([[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]).

The honest boundary: MOIA's model uses *digital* curbside stops and no built infrastructure — it validates the fleet and operations; the Hub City contributes the spatial layer MOIA does not address ([[Hub as Place]]).
