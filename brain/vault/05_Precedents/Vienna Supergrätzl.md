# Vienna Supergrätzl

The German-speaking, tactical-to-permanent precedent. Vienna's first superblock pilot (Favoriten, 2022) used the cheap toolkit — planters, one-way loops, modal filters — to stop through-traffic across a cluster of streets around the Herzgasse school, then **made the temporary measures permanent** (opened May 2026): 60 new trees, 94 added green spaces, new pedestrian zones, seating and community gardens ([[Superblock]], [[Modal Filter]]).

Two things transfer directly to Wolfsburg. First, the **phasing pathway**: test with reversible interventions, let residents live the change, then fix in concrete what proved popular — the consent recipe in built form ([[Tactical Urbanism]], [[Imposition Without Consent]]). Second, the **toolkit itself**: one-way internal loops plus point filters is precisely the circulation instrument proposed for the interior streets of Wolfsburg's five zones ([[Five-Zone Model]]).

As an Austrian project in a German-speaking planning culture, it is also the precedent a Wolfsburg jury recognises without translation — proof the superblock family has crossed the language border.
