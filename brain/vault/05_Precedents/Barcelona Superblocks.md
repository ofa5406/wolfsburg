# Barcelona Superblocks

The reclamation precedent with the strongest numbers. Barcelona groups ~3×3 blocks (~400×400 m) into *superilles*: through-traffic leaves the interior streets, internal speeds drop to ~10 km/h, and the freed roadway becomes plazas, play and planting — the car-land-to-public-life move at the heart of the Wolfsburg thesis, executed at metropolitan scale ([[Superblock]]).

The health modelling is the headline: full build-out could prevent **~667 premature deaths a year** (air 291, noise 163, heat 117), cut city NO₂ ~24%, and add ~200 days of life expectancy per resident ([[Barcelona Health Dividend]]). The spatial arithmetic is just as quotable — up to 60–70% of street space reclaimed while removing only a quarter of traffic ([[Superblock Street Reclaim Percentages]]).

Two honesty flags travel with the precedent: the health numbers are *modelled* projections for the full plan, only partially realised; and the programme began as tactical-urbanism paint-and-planters in Gràcia before becoming structural — the trial-to-permanent pathway the project's phasing borrows ([[Tactical Urbanism]]). Barcelona's chess tables and generous fixed furniture also seeded the social layer of the hub palette ([[Game Table]]).
