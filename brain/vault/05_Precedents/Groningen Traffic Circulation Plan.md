# Groningen Traffic Circulation Plan

The project's DNA. On the night of **19 September 1977**, Groningen split its centre into **four sectors**: cars could no longer cross between them — to reach the next sector a driver had to return to the ring road and re-enter — while bicycles and pedestrians kept full permeability through new continuous routes. Filtered permeability, invented at city scale, overnight ([[Filtered Permeability]]).

The results defined the genre: inner-city car traffic roughly **halved**; today **~60% of all trips are by bicycle**, among the highest shares of any city its size; the centre grew commercially stronger; and the plan became the template copied across the Netherlands and beyond.

Wolfsburg's structure *is* this precedent scaled up: five zones for Groningen's four sectors, restricted zone entrances for the ring re-entry rule ([[Five-Zone Model]]). The lessons the project quotes: cars are re-routed, never banned — every address stays reachable; the cycle network must stay continuous exactly where the car network is cut ([[Rad Network]]); and a decisive switch-on beats years of half-measures.
