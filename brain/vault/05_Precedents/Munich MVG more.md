# Munich MVG more

The large-German-city operator model. Munich's MVG *more* bundles bike-share (MVG Rad), e-bikes, car-share, public transport, taxi and EV charging at physical **Mobilitätsstationen** commissioned by the City of Munich and run under the transit operator's single brand.

Two transfers to Wolfsburg. First, **institutional**: a municipal transit operator can own the whole multimodal offer — one commissioning authority, one brand, one app — which is the governance shape the Hub City assumes for its municipal-VW operator, and the structural answer to the fragmentation that makes [[MaaS Is Governance-Hard]] elsewhere ([[Integrated Ticketing]]). Second, **semiotic**: like Vienna's WienMobil ([[Vienna WienMobil]]), Munich shows that a consistent station identity across many sites is what turns scattered mobility offers into a legible network — the precedent trail behind Wolfsburg's insistence on one design language for 68 hubs ([[System Identity]]).

As a precedent it is corroborating rather than primary: the brain cites Bremen for tiers and outcomes, Vienna for element logic, Munich for the operator-brand pattern at Germany's largest-city scale ([[Mobility Hub]]).
