# Zurich VBZ Tram Shelters

The quality benchmark for the humblest element in the palette. Zurich's tram and bus shelters (Verkehrsbetriebe Zürich) are designed as **proper enclosed, heated waiting rooms** — glazed, warm, generously dimensioned — not minimal canopies bolted to a pole. Waiting for transit in Zurich is treated as time spent in a public interior, and the infrastructure is built to that standard.

The precedent disciplines the Wolfsburg palette against its most likely failure mode: value-engineering the waiting experience down to a roof. The [[Public Transport Shed]] and [[Heated Waiting Zone]] elements cite Zurich as their aspiration — in a post-car city, the shelter *is* the front room of the mobility system, and its material quality signals whether the alternative to the car is serious ([[Smooth Usage]]).

The deeper argument Zurich carries: transit infrastructure quality is a *statement about who the system respects*. A heated shelter at a February shift change tells the night worker the system was built for them — the equity table, rendered in glass and warm air ([[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]], [[Catalyst Not Shelter]]).
