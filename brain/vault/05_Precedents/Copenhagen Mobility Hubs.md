# Copenhagen Mobility Hubs

The research-led, regional variant — and the direct ancestor of the project's beacon. Greater Copenhagen's living-lab programme (ShareDiMobiHub) deploys **flexible micro-hubs at suburban stations**: e-bikes, car-share and EV charging under **modular solar canopies**, with **wayfinding pylons — pictograms plus LED — visible from about 100 metres**, standing on permeable paving with green swales for stormwater.

Nearly every element maps one-to-one onto the Wolfsburg palette: their pylon is the [[Vertical Beacon]] (readable from 50 m in the finer-grained network); their solar canopy is the [[Solar Canopy]]; their permeable-paving-and-swale ground is the [[Permeable Surface Zone]] and [[Planting Bed and Bioswale]]. The plug-and-play modularity — municipalities reconfigure layouts as demand evolves — is the [[Kit-of-Parts]] proposition with a Danish accent.

Copenhagen also had an earlier city hub programme defining tiers and element palettes with a consistent design language ([[Hub Tier System]]). The transfer lesson: a hub network reads as one system when its identity elements are standardised and its layouts are not — uniform beacon, contextual composition ([[System Identity]]).
