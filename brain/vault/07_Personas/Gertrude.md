# Gertrude

Persona 04: **Gertrude, 85, retired** — the persona the equity critique says systems like this forget, placed deliberately at the end of the cycle as its proof of coverage.

Before: after her leg gave out she stopped driving; every trip meant asking her daughter. After: an autonomous pod arrives at her door — no transfers, no steps. **Market on Tuesdays again.**

Her story answers the hardest accessibility charge — that shared mobility is designed for fit young app-users ([[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]]). The design commitments behind her three sentences are specific: door-to-door on-demand service where fixed stops fail ([[Micro-Pod]], [[Demand-Responsive Transit]]), wheelchair-accessible flat-floor vehicles in the fleet ([[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]), booking by phone as well as app, and step-free hub thresholds with tactile guidance at every tier ([[Tactile Paving Strips]]).

"Market on Tuesdays again" is the project's definition of success at its most concrete: mobility as participation restored — not trips per vehicle, but a Tuesday returned ([[Public Life]], [[Human Scale]]).
