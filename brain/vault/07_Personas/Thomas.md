# Thomas

Persona 01 of the exhibition cycle — first in reading order because he *is* the town: **Thomas, 44, engineer at the VW plant.**

Before: thirty minutes in traffic every morning; a new car every three years — it just felt like the way things were. After: an autonomous bus from the L-hub to the factory gate in eighteen minutes. **He helps build the fleet — and rides it.**

That closing line carries the project's whole political strategy in nine words. Thomas is the person the company-town objection says will resist ([[Risk — Company Town Politics]], [[Wolfsburg Privileged Identity Study]]) — and his story inverts the threat: the post-car system is not what takes his livelihood, it is what his livelihood *makes* ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]], [[Wolfsburg as Testbed]]). His commute rides the trunk of the system, the [[Autonomous Bus]] on the [[Commuter Corridor]], through the case-study hub ([[VW Factory Gate]]).

Where [[Anna]] is the engineered proof journey, Thomas is its public face on the kiosk — before/after in one breath, for a visitor with thirty seconds ([[Summaery Exhibition]]).
