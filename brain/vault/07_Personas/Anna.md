# Anna

The proof framework's protagonist: a VW worker whose **home-to-factory-gate journey without a private car** is the project's third required demonstration — time, transfers and experience, documented step by step ([[Proof Framework]]).

Anna's journey is designed as the system's integration test. It begins at a dwelling inside the zones, walks the 150–250 m to an S-hub ([[S-Hub]]), boards a pooled vehicle on the trunk network, and arrives at the [[VW Factory Gate]] hub inside the shift wave — the single hardest condition the fleet faces ([[VW Shift Wave]]). If her trip beats or matches the car door-to-door, the modal-shift argument closes; if it doesn't, the design has work to do. The agent-based ambition is to simulate her literally ([[VW Shift Wave Simulation]]).

She is also the equity table's first row made human — the shift worker whose commute the system must serve at 6 a.m. and 10 p.m. alike ([[Shift Workers Face Double Commutes]], [[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]]). The exhibition's persona cycle ([[Thomas]], [[Sabine]], [[Lukas]], [[Gertrude]]) generalises what Anna proves.
