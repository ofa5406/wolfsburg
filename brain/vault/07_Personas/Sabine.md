# Sabine

Persona 02: **Sabine, 38, office worker with two children** — the household economics persona, and the trip-chaining case the equity table flags as hardest after the shift worker ([[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]]).

Before: two family cars — €400 a month, parking stress, mornings of traffic and running late. After: e-bike to the S-hub, shuttle to the office in twelve minutes — **and the kids ride the same shuttle to school on their own.**

Two arguments live in her story. The financial one is the €233-per-month finding personalised: dissolving the second car (then the first) returns real money to a family budget ([[German Households Spend 233 Euro Monthly on Cars]], [[Access over Ownership]]). The deeper one is the last clause — children moving independently. A city safe and legible enough that a parent stops being a chauffeur returns hours to Sabine and autonomy to the kids, which is [[Lukas]]'s story told from the parent's side ([[Trip Chaining]], [[E-Cargo Bike]]).

Her chain — walk, [[E-bike Docking Rack]], [[Shuttle Pod]] — is the [[Trip Chaining]] concept as a morning routine ([[S-Hub]]).
