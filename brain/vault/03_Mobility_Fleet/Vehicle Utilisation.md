# Vehicle Utilisation

The inversion at the core of the whole system: a privately owned car is parked **about 95% of the time** ([[Cars Parked 95 Percent of the Time]]), while a shared vehicle is *in motion* most of the day. Utilisation is where the land saving comes from — high duty cycles mean few vehicles, few vehicles mean little storage, little storage means the parking estate comes free.

The concept disciplines the fleet math both ways. It powers the efficiency claims (the ITF Lisbon fleet was so small precisely because its vehicles never rested), and it forbids overclaiming: a shared EV cannot run at 100% — downtime for charging and rebalancing must be budgeted, which is why the [[Charging Window Strategy]] and [[Fleet Rebalancing]] are named operating conditions of the sizing rather than afterthoughts.
