# Autonomous Bus

The fleet's heavy lifter: **33 high-capacity autonomous buses** on fixed trunk routes, carrying the crowd-scale flows no smaller vehicle class can absorb. In the shift-wave arithmetic each bus moving ~30 passengers per run and cycling three times an hour clears thousands of workers — the backbone of the [[VW Shift Wave]] service alongside pooled shuttles.

Buses earn their fixed routes: where demand is thick and predictable (factory corridor, centre-to-station), a schedule beats an algorithm. Their hub interface is the [[AV Drop-off and Pickup Zone]] at M and L tiers, and their per-vehicle economics improve most from automation since driver cost dominates bus operations — the honest lesson of BerlKönig's shutdown applied at the largest vehicle scale ([[BerlKönig]]).
