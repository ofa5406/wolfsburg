# Pooling Efficiency

The single most important operational fact for Wolfsburg, from MOIA's own simulation work: **pooling efficiency rises roughly logarithmically with trip density.** Dense, concentrated demand pools far better than thin, scattered demand — each additional request in a corridor is ever more likely to match an existing route.

The finding sorts cities into good and bad hosts for shared mobility, and Wolfsburg lands in the best seat: the factory commute concentrates thousands of trips into one corridor on one clock, pushing pooling toward its theoretical maximum exactly when capacity is scarcest. The same finding warns honestly about the system's edges — low-density districts and off-peak hours pool poorly, which is where the micro-pod's individual service and the shared EV absorb the thin demand that pooling cannot.
