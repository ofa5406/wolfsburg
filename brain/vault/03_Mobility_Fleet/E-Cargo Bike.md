# E-Cargo Bike

The fleet's muscle-powered logistics class: **131 electric cargo bikes** for shopping, deliveries and families — the mode that covers the "but how do I carry things?" objection at one-hundredth of a car's footprint. Docked at the [[Cargo Bike Dock]] and extended by the [[Cargo Bike Lending Library]] where demand concentrates, e-cargo bikes serve 6–10 rentals a day in the capacity model, on par with active bike-share systems.

The class does double duty as freight infrastructure: inside the filtered zones, courier cargo-bike runs replace van circuits, feeding the [[Parcel Pickup Lockers]] and keeping delivery traffic within the system rather than against it. Electric assist flattens Wolfsburg's distances, making the cargo trip a default rather than an athletic feat.
