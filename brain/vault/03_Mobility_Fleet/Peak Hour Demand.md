# Peak Hour Demand

The stress test: **~9,000 trips in the worst hour**, 9% of daily demand — a normal urban peaking factor (typical peak hours carry 8–12% of daily trips). At fleet level this means ~12 trips per vehicle in the peak hour, a vehicle completing a trip roughly every five minutes: easy for e-bikes and micro-pods on short hops, and misleadingly modest for buses and shuttles whose every "trip" carries many passengers.

The peak's composition matters more than its size — much of it is the [[VW Shift Wave]], which is predictable, concentrated and directional, and therefore the *favourable* kind of peak: vehicles can be pre-positioned and pooled at maximum efficiency. The fleet is sized to this hour; the other twenty-three are margin, charging windows and rebalancing time.
