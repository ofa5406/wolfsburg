# Pre-positioning

Moving vehicles *before* demand appears instead of after: because the [[VW Shift Wave]] runs on a published clock, the [[Dispatch Optimiser]] can stage buses, pods and shuttles at the right hubs minutes ahead of the surge, eliminating the dispatch latency that makes on-demand systems feel slow.

Pre-positioning is the cheapest form of [[Fleet Rebalancing]] there is — repositioning toward *predicted* demand rather than chasing realised demand. In general DRT operations, rebalancing and empty runs ([[Deadheading]]) consume 14–28% of vehicle-kilometres; a predictable wave lets most of that budget be spent once, deliberately, in the quiet minutes before the pulse rather than reactively during it.

The concept scales down too: e-bike docks are topped up before school-run hours, shared EVs drift toward the station before weekend mornings, and micro-pods gather near the stadium before matches end. Every predictable rhythm in the [[Demand Basis]] is an invitation to pre-position — the fleet behaves less like a taxi swarm and more like a tide that arrives on schedule.
