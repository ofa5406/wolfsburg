# Shuttle Pod

The fleet's pooling specialist: **55 autonomous shuttle pods**, roughly six-seat vehicles with flexible on-demand routing — the MOIA model made municipal. Pods run [[Demand-Responsive Transit]]: an optimiser clusters trip requests, inserts pickups and drop-offs on the fly, and trades passenger wait and detour against vehicle-kilometres.

The class's direct precedent is VW's own [[MOIA]] operation — ~450 electric six-seaters serving Hamburg, ~60% of peak trips pooled in Hannover, and from 2026 the production Level-4 ID. Buzz AD — which is why the pod is the fleet's most credible autonomous claim. In the capacity table each pod delivers 60–90 passenger-trips a day through pooling, and in the shift wave pods cycling four times an hour add over a thousand seats to the corridor ([[Shift Wave Capacity Check]]).
