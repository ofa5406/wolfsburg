# Daily Trip Demand

The demand side of the fleet equation: **~100,000 trips per day** in the project area, generated by roughly **17,000 residents, 18,000 VW workers and 17,000 daily visitors**. The trip total assumes each person generates around three daily trips — consistent with German national travel survey averages (MiD 2017) — with the worker and visitor populations contributing their Wolfsburg-side trip ends.

The 100k figure is the denominator of every capacity claim, so its composition is deliberately traceable: residents' everyday mobility, the plant's commuting mass, and the visitor economy of the Autostadt, stadium and city centre. Peak concentration is captured separately — about 9% of daily demand lands in the worst hour ([[Peak Hour Demand]]), within the normal 8–12% urban range, meaning the demand curve is heavy but not pathological.
