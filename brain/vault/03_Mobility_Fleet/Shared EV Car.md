# Shared EV Car

The fleet's honest concession to the car's remaining strengths: **175 shared electric cars** for longer trips, families, luggage and destinations beyond the network. Station-based at the [[Shared EV Bay]] of M and L hubs, they follow the Bremen model — the German car-sharing pattern with the strongest measured substitution effect, around eleven private cars replaced per shared one in the national association's data ([[One Shared Car Replaces 11 Private Cars]]).

Shared EVs run the lowest cycle rate in the fleet (4–8 bookings a day, longer durations), so their contribution is measured in coverage of the *hard* trips rather than volume. Their existence is strategic honesty: a system that pretends no one ever needs a car invites disbelief; a system that pools the need into 175 vehicles proves the point instead.
