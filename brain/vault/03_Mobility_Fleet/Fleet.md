# Fleet

The locked vehicle stock of the Hub City: **763 shared vehicles** replacing tens of thousands of private cars in central Wolfsburg. Composition, fixed by group decision on June 11: **33 [[Autonomous Bus]]** (high-capacity trunk routes), **55 [[Shuttle Pod]]** (flexible on-demand pooling), **369 [[Micro-Pod]]** (individual and small-group last-mile), **175 [[Shared EV Car]]** (longer trips, families), and **131 [[E-Cargo Bike]]** (shopping, logistics, active users).

The fleet is sized for roughly 100,000 trips per day with a 9,000-trip peak hour ([[Fleet Sizing]]), on a demand basis of 17,000 residents, 18,000 workers and 17,000 daily visitors. All vehicles are electric, shared and increasingly autonomous; they live nowhere — docked briefly at hubs, charged and maintained at the six L-hubs, and otherwise in motion, which is the entire point. A competing figure of 1,273 vehicles exists in the web tool and is tracked as [[Fleet Number Conflict]].
