# Micro-Pod

The fleet's most numerous vehicle: **369 autonomous micro-pods** for one or two passengers — the last-mile workhorse shuttling between S-hubs, M-hubs and the fine grain of the street network. Micro-pods are the high-cycle end of the capacity table: 25–40 short hops per vehicle per day, delivering ~12,000 daily trips, more than any other class.

The micro-pod answers the trips that defeat conventional transit: the 700-metre hop with shopping bags, the mobility-limited resident's dock-to-door leg, the rainy-evening return from the M-hub. Its street interface is deliberately light — the [[Micro-Pod Zone]] is paint and paving, repeatable 68 times — and its operating domain is exactly the filtered, low-speed interior of the [[Five-Zone Model]], where Level-4 autonomy is most tractable today.
