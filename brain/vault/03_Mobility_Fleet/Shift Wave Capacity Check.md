# Shift Wave Capacity Check

The back-of-envelope that keeps the shift-wave claim honest: 10,000 workers in 60 minutes are cleared by **high-capacity pooled modes on a corridor, not by 10,000 individual pods.** Reference arithmetic: 33 autonomous buses × ~30 effective passengers × 3 cycles ≈ 3,000; 55 shuttle pods × 6 passengers × 4 cycles ≈ 1,300; the remainder carried by heavy rail and tram trunk capacity plus staggered shift sub-waves.

The explicit framing — corridor pooling supported by rail, with sub-wave staggering — is called out in the research as "the difference between a credible and an absurd claim." The check also quantifies why pre-positioning matters: dispatch latency, not vehicle count, is what would break a one-hour wave, and a predictable wave allows the fleet to be staged before it breaks.
