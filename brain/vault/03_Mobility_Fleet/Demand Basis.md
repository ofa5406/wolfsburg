# Demand Basis

The population arithmetic underneath every fleet number: the Hub City serves **17,000 residents, 18,000 VW workers and 17,000 daily visitors** — three roughly equal demand blocks with completely different rhythms. Residents generate all-day, all-purpose trips; workers arrive and leave in [[VW Shift Wave]] pulses; visitors cluster around Autostadt, the stadium, phaeno and the centre on afternoons and weekends.

From this basis the project derives **~100,000 trips per day** ([[Daily Trip Demand]]) and a **9,000-trip peak hour** ([[Peak Hour Demand]]), which in turn size the 763-vehicle [[Fleet]]. The three-block structure matters more than the total: a fleet sized only for residents would drown in the shift wave, and one sized for the wave alone would idle all day. The [[Modal Distribution Model]] spreads each block across the five modes according to trip length and purpose.

Stating the demand basis explicitly is part of the project's proof discipline — the jury can disagree with an assumption, but it is written down, sourced and adjustable.
