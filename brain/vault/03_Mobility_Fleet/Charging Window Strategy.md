# Charging Window Strategy

The energy logistics of a 763-vehicle electric fleet: charging happens **at the six L-hubs during off-peak troughs and overnight**, scheduled into the valleys between demand waves. The planning maxim from depot practice: think in **megawatt-hours over a dwell window, not megawatts** — utilities care about energy delivered across time, and disciplined dwell scheduling can shrink grid build-out from years to months.

The strategy exploits the same predictability as everything else in Wolfsburg's system: shift waves fix when vehicles are busy, therefore also when they are free to charge, and the [[EV Fleet Charging at Scale]] infrastructure is sized to those known windows rather than to a worst-case simultaneous plug-in. Grid capacity, aggregate demand and storage buffering are examined in the future-projections research and logged among the sizing's operating conditions.
