# Urban Proof

The project's methodological stance, crystallised by the June 4 consultation instruction: **"prove the system, not just images."** An urban proposal earns belief not through renders but through checkable chains of evidence — coverage maps that show who is actually served, fleet mathematics that survive a peak-hour stress test, persona journeys with real times and transfers, land audits with measurable areas.

The stance produced the [[Proof Framework]]: six demonstrations (coverage, fleet sizing, persona journey, car-land map, street sections, phased timeline) that together constitute the argument. It also disciplines honesty — numbers that conflict are flagged ([[Fleet Number Conflict]]), methods that are aspirational rather than implemented are documented ([[Methodology vs Code Gap]]), and uncertainty is stated. In a studio graded partly on AI-augmented workflow, the proof stance extends to the tools: every analysis is reproducible from data and code, not assembled by hand.
