# Hub as Infrastructure

The second nature of the [[Mobility Hub]]: the hub as an operational node in a fleet system. Under this reading a hub is defined by what it does for vehicles and throughput — docking and charging points, AV drop-off geometry, dispatch and rebalancing logic, interchange between modes, maintenance access, and proximity to the parking structures that make fast fleet servicing possible.

The web tool's placement algorithms embody this reading: locations derive from existing multi-storey and underground car parks, coverage radii are service ranges rather than walking distances. The tension with [[Hub as Place]] is documented rather than hidden — it is the project's central internal debate, resolved on 2026-06-17 by separating *where* a hub goes (infrastructure logic may decide) from *what* a hub is (the place typology is protected on the surface).
