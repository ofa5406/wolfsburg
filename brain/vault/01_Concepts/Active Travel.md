# Active Travel

Walking and cycling as transport — the modes powered by human muscle, and the quiet majority shareholders of any successful post-car system. Every hub journey begins and ends on foot; every filtered street is, first of all, a better place to walk and ride. Active travel also carries the public-health dividend: population-level physical activity embedded in daily routine, cleaner air, fewer traffic injuries.

The project's active-travel infrastructure is layered: the [[Five-Zone Model]] removes through traffic from the walking environment; the [[Rad Network]] builds a bike-priority web connecting hubs, districts and the VW plant; the [[Street Transformation Sections]] spend reclaimed carriageway on footpaths and protected lanes first. The LTN evidence base ([[LTN Active Travel Evidence]]) documents that this kind of network surgery measurably increases walking and cycling among residents.
