# From Parking City to Hub City

The project thesis in one move. Wolfsburg gives a disproportionate share of its land to private cars, amplified by Volkswagen's commuting patterns; if mobility is reorganised around a shared, multimodal, autonomous system anchored in hubs, that land becomes available for public life, greenery, and housing.

The thesis is deliberately stated as a substitution of organising elements, not a ban: **parking is the infrastructure of private ownership; hubs are the infrastructure of shared mobility.** Everything in the project — the 68-hub network, the 5-zone traffic structure, the fleet sizing, the persona journeys, the reclaimed-land drawings — exists to prove this one substitution is workable in the most car-saturated city in Germany, which is exactly what makes it the strongest possible testbed.
