# Eyes on the Street

[[Jane Jacobs]]'s 1961 shorthand for natural surveillance: streets are kept safe not by police or cameras but by the continuous, casual presence of people — residents at windows, shopkeepers at doors, passers-by on errands. The precondition is fine-grained mixed use, active ground floors, and enough foot traffic across the day.

The concept has direct consequences for hub design. A mobility hub used around the clock — commuters at dawn, shoppers midday, returning workers at night — supplies exactly the continuous presence Jacobs described, which is why the palette pairs mobility elements with a kiosk, seating and lighting rather than leaving the hub a bare dock. Conversely, an unwatched hub decays fast. Round-the-clock legibility and the [[Ambient Responsive Lighting]] element are the project's operational answers, especially for night-shift workers heading to the [[Volkswagen]] plant.
