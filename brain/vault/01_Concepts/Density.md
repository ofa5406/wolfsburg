# Density

The concentration of people, homes and destinations per unit of land — the variable that makes every other post-car mechanism viable. Shared fleets need enough demand per hub to keep vehicles busy; shops need enough walk-in customers; transit needs enough riders per stop. Below a density threshold, sharing collapses into inconvenience and the private car wins by default.

Wolfsburg's density is moderate and uneven, which shapes the design: the project concentrates its proof on the central districts (the nine core neighbourhoods around Stadtmitte) where roughly 17,000 residents, 18,000 workers and 17,000 daily visitors generate about 100,000 trips per day — enough to justify the fleet. Parking-land recycling raises density where it matters: each dissolved lot becomes housing or programme, feeding demand back into the hubs that replaced it.
