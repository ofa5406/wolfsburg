# Bike Sharing

Public fleets of bicycles — increasingly e-bikes — available for short-term use from docks or zones. Docked systems suit hub networks: the dock is a fixed, weather-protected, chargeable element, and its presence advertises the whole mobility offer. E-bikes specifically flatten Wolfsburg's distances and headwinds, extending the comfortable cycling radius to around five kilometres — enough to reach the [[Volkswagen]] plant from most central districts.

In the fleet, 131 e-cargo bikes serve the encumbered trips ordinary bikes cannot: shopping, deliveries, transporting children ([[E-Cargo Bike]]). The [[E-bike Docking Rack]] is a mandatory element at every tier, making bike share the most ubiquitous service in the network — present even at the smallest [[S-Hub]], where it is often the entire mobility offer plus a bench and a beacon.
