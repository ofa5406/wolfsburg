# Behaviour Change

The psychology underneath modal shift. Travel is mostly habit, not choice: the daily mode decision is made once and replayed automatically, which is why information campaigns barely move it. The levers that work are **defaults** (make the sustainable option the path of least resistance), **[[Moments of Change]]** (habits are renegotiated when life changes — new home, new job, new child), and **experience** (a single good trial run beats any brochure — the logic of [[Tactical Urbanism]] pilots).

Wolfsburg adds a sociological layer: a company town whose identity, employment and pensions are car-built ([[German Car Culture]]). The project's behavioural bet is that pride can migrate — from *making* cars to *pioneering* what comes after them — and that the first habit to convert is the most collective one: the factory commute, where one employer's shift schedule ([[VW Shift Wave]]) synchronises thousands of identical decisions.
