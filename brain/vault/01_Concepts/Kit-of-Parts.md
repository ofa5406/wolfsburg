# Kit-of-Parts

The design method behind the hub typology: define a finite catalogue of standardised elements — docks, canopies, benches, planting modules, info terminals — and compose every individual hub from that shared kit. Modularity buys three things at once: **consistency** (all 68 hubs are recognisably one family), **adaptability** (each site composes only what it needs), and **buildability** (elements are prefabricated, budgeted and maintained as products, not one-off designs).

The method is standard in contemporary mobility-hub practice and central to the project: the [[Hub Toolpalette]] catalogues ~45 elements across nine categories, with per-tier requirements (mandatory at L, optional at S, and so on). The kit exists physically as a Rhino block library ([[Rhino Toolpalette Kit]]) from which the sample hub scenes and the [[Hub Viewer]] are built — the same modularity running from concept to geometry to exhibit.
