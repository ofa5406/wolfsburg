# Wolfsburg as Testbed

The project's central reframe: Wolfsburg is not a risky place to attempt post-car mobility — it is **the single most logical pilot city in the world for it**. The company building the vehicles ([[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]), the fleet-management software, the simulation tools ([[MIA Mobility Impact Analyzer]]) and the operating model ([[MOIA]]) is headquartered there, and has publicly bet its future on exactly this business ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]]).

The city's apparent liabilities convert to assets under this frame. Highest car density in Germany → the most meaningful before/after on Earth ([[Wolfsburg Highest Car Density]]). The brutal shift wave → the densest predictable demand any pooled system could ask for ([[VW Shift Wave]], [[Commuter Corridor]]). Company-town identity → a single institutional partner able to move at company speed ([[Employer-Anchored Commuting]]).

The frame's political function is decisive: it converts the project from an attack on the town's livelihood into the town's **second economic identity** — the world's reference installation of what VW sells next ([[Post-Car City in VW's Hometown]], [[VW Cuts 35000 Jobs]]).
