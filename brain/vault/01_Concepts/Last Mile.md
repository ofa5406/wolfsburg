# Last Mile

The final leg between a transit stop or hub and the actual front door — historically the gap that private cars exploit, because trunk transit cannot serve every street. If the last mile is unsolved, people drive the whole journey; if it is solved, the trunk system captures the trip.

The project's answer is the S tier: 43 [[S-Hub]] nodes with 150–250 m catchments push shared access into the capillaries of the street network, offering an e-bike dock, a micro-pod stop, a bench and a beacon — the minimum viable hub. Micro-pods and e-cargo bikes are the last-mile vehicles of the [[Fleet]], handling encumbered trips (shopping, children) that walking cannot. Coverage analysis targets 80–90% of residents within 300 m of an S- or M-hub ([[Hub Coverage]]), which is the quantified promise that no one is left outside the system.
