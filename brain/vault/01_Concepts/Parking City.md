# Parking City

The diagnosis. Wolfsburg is a city organised around the storage of private cars: surface lots, multi-storey garages, on-street parking, and streets sized for car movement between them. Built as Volkswagen's company town, its urban form literally encodes car ownership — it has the highest car density of any German city. In the project's reading, "Parking City" is not an insult but a precise description of an organising principle: every household stores its own idle vehicle near its front door, so car storage is dispersed into every street and plot, and public space is what remains after parking is served.

The project's counter-model is the [[Hub City]]. The land audit that makes the diagnosis visible is the [[Car-Land Composite Map]], and the theory that explains how parking silently produces this form comes from [[Donald Shoup]].
