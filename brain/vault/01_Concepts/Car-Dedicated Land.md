# Car-Dedicated Land

The total urban surface currently allocated to private cars: surface parking lots, multi-storey garages, on-street parking lanes, access ramps, and the share of carriageway sized for private-car movement rather than access. The project's audit of this land in Wolfsburg is the empirical basis of the [[Parking City]] diagnosis, and its single-image expression is the [[Car-Land Composite Map]] — the rhetorical anchor of the whole submission.

The category matters because it makes an invisible subsidy visible. Parking is experienced as background, but measured as land it is a vast, contiguous estate. Paris's rhetoric applies: cars carry a small share of trips while occupying about half of street space ([[Paris 13 Percent Trips 50 Percent Space]]). Mapping car-dedicated land converts a cultural argument into a measurable one.
