# Public Transport

The fixed-route, high-capacity layer of the network — in Wolfsburg, the bus system, joined in the project by 33 autonomous buses on trunk corridors. Public transport is the only mode that scales to genuine crowd movements; no amount of micro-pods substitutes for a full bus at shift change, which is why the [[VW Shift Wave]] analysis assigns the trunk flow to buses and pods in bulk.

The project treats existing bus stops as inherited assets: the S-hub placement logic grows the new network out of the current stop pattern (stops are hub candidates in the [[Intermodal Hub Algorithm]]), so the Hub City upgrades rather than discards the transit map. At hub surfaces, the [[Public Transport Shed]] element gives waiting the dignity the palette insists on — enclosed, heated at M and L tiers, following the Zurich VBZ precedent ([[Zurich VBZ Tram Shelters]]).
