# Wayfinding

The design discipline of making navigation self-evident: knowing where you are, what is offered, and where to go next, without asking or studying. In a hub network wayfinding does double duty — it guides journeys *and* brands the system. A consistent visual identity across 68 hubs (one beacon, one ground colour, one information design) makes the whole city read as a single service, the way a metro map makes scattered stations feel like one machine.

Precedent systems prove the point: Bremen's mobil.punkt pillar, Munich's MVG station identity, Vienna's WienMobil signage. The palette dedicates a category to it — [[Toolpalette Category — Wayfinding and Accessibility]] — including the [[Hub Identity Marker]], [[Directional Lines]] in the ground field, [[Real-time Info Terminal]], and [[Tactile Paving Strips]] extending legibility to blind and low-vision users.
