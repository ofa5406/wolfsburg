# Moments of Change

The behavioural-science finding that travel habits are renegotiated at life disruptions — moving house, changing jobs, having a child, a car breaking down — and are nearly immovable in between. Interventions timed to these windows (a welcome mobility package for new residents, a trial pass when a workplace relocates) convert at rates that blanket campaigns never reach.

The concept scales up: a *city* can have moments of change. Wolfsburg's are legible years in advance — VW's electrification restructuring, plant conversions, retirement waves, new quarters coming online — and the [[Implementation Timeline]] is deliberately phased to ride them. The disruption that a shrinking combustion workforce brings is, in this frame, not only a threat to the city but the largest habit-window it will ever get: the moment to offer thousands of households a genuinely better default than the second car.
