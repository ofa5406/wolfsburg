# Modal Filter

The physical device that produces [[Filtered Permeability]]: a point in the street network that lets pedestrians, cyclists and sometimes buses pass while blocking private cars. Classic forms are bollards, planter groups, short bus gates, and camera-enforced restrictions; at the scale of the Wolfsburg plan, the restricted **zone entrances** of the [[Five-Zone Model]] perform the same function at city-sector size.

A modal filter is cheap, reversible, and surgical — it changes network topology without demolishing anything, which is why it became the workhorse of London's Low-Traffic Neighbourhood programme and of tactical circulation plans like Ghent's. In the project's phasing, filters are early-phase moves: they can lock in the zone logic years before the hub architecture is complete, and their reversibility is an honest answer to public scepticism.
