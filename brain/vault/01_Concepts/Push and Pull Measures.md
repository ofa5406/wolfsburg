# Push and Pull Measures

The two-handed strategy of modal shift. **Push** measures make car use less attractive: parking removal and pricing, filtered permeability, slower internal streets, reallocation of road space. **Pull** measures make alternatives more attractive: dense hub coverage, fast reliable shared services, dignified waiting environments, protected cycling, and one-account MaaS simplicity.

Behavioural research is unambiguous that the pair must operate together — pull alone is ignored by comfortable drivers, push alone breeds resentment without offering an exit. The project's structure encodes the pairing spatially: every zone-entrance restriction (push) is matched by a hub within walking distance (pull); every removed parking lane (push) funds visible public-space gains on the same street (pull). Sequencing them honestly across the [[Implementation Timeline]] is what separates a transition from a provocation.
