# Shared Mobility

Transportation services whose vehicles are used sequentially or simultaneously by many people rather than owned by one household: car sharing, bike and scooter share, ridepooling, demand-responsive shuttles, and their autonomous descendants. The economic engine is utilisation — a private car sits idle roughly 95% of the time, while a shared vehicle can serve dozens of trips a day, so a small fleet substitutes for a large parked stock.

Adoption evidence supports the substitution: in Bremen each shared car replaces around sixteen private ones and about a third of users gave up or never bought a car ([[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]]). In the project, shared mobility is the operating principle of the whole 763-vehicle [[Fleet]], and the [[Mobility Hub]] is its physical storefront — the fixed, trustworthy place where a fluid system meets the street.
