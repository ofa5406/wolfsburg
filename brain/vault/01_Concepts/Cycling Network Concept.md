# Cycling Network Concept

The principle that cycling becomes mass transport only as a **network**, never as isolated lanes: continuous, direct, safe routes connecting where people live to where they go, with protected intersections and legible priority. The Dutch orthodoxy — grid spacing, design speed, separation by traffic volume — plus the newer copenhagenised corridor logic of Paris's Voies Lyonnaises and the Plan Vélo.

The project implements the principle as the [[Rad Network]]: a Dijkstra-routed, bike-friendliness-weighted web over Wolfsburg's street graph, connecting hubs to the destinations that matter (Order-1: VW gates, centre, stations; Order-2: schools, historic sites; Order-3: neighbourhood targets), with dashed segments marking gaps that need infrastructure. The official municipal Radwege dataset grounds the analysis in what exists today, and e-bikes extend the network's reach to plant-commute distances.
