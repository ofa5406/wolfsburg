# Street-Space Reallocation

The practice of redistributing carriageway and parking lanes to walking, cycling, greenery and staying activities. The spatial-justice case was crystallised in Paris: cars account for roughly 13% of trips but occupy about half of all street space, so reallocation simply rebalances the street toward how people actually move ([[Paris 13 Percent Trips 50 Percent Space]]).

Practice guidance turns the principle into drawn detail: NACTO's *Urban Street Design Guide* codifies the complete street — lane diets, parklets, plazas, protected bike lanes — while ITDP's *TOD Standard* connects reduced parking to transit-oriented nodes at network scale, and [[Jan Gehl]]'s public-life surveys measure the result. In the project, reallocation is drawn concretely in the [[Street Transformation Sections]] for Kleiststraße and its companion street, where removed parking becomes footpath, trees and dwelling space.
