# Car Sharing

Short-term access to cars owned by an operator or cooperative — station-based (return to a fixed spot) or free-floating (leave anywhere in a zone). Station-based sharing, the model relevant here, integrates naturally with hubs: the shared EV bay is simply one more element in the palette, and the fixed station gives the service a legible, trustworthy address.

Car sharing carries the strongest substitution evidence in the whole shared-mobility family: Bremen's programme — the German benchmark — shows each shared car replacing about sixteen private ones, six-thousand-plus vehicles removed, and an effect valued at roughly €200 million of avoided parking investment ([[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]], [[Bremen mobil.punkt]]). In the project, 175 shared EVs cover the trips the rest of the system handles poorly: families with luggage, night journeys, out-of-town destinations — the honest residual of car usefulness.
