# Mobility as a Service

MaaS: the integration of multiple transport modes — transit, shared cars, bikes, pods, taxis — into a single user-facing service with unified planning, booking and payment, usually via subscription or account. The user buys *mobility outcomes* ("get me there by 8:00") rather than mode-specific tickets; a digital platform composes each journey from whatever the network offers.

The concept was formulated in Finland in the mid-2010s, with Helsinki's Whim app as the canonical first deployment ([[MaaS Origins in Finland]]). MaaS matters to the project as the invisible layer that makes 68 physical hubs behave as **one system**: the same account unlocks an e-bike at an [[S-Hub]], a micro-pod at an [[M-Hub]], and a shared EV at an [[L-Hub]]. Without MaaS integration, a hub network degenerates into disconnected rental points; with it, intermodal journeys become single decisions.
