# Woonerf

The Dutch "living street" (literally *living yard*), pioneered in Delft around 1970: a street legally and physically redesigned so that pedestrians, children and cyclists have priority over cars, which may enter only at walking pace as guests. No continuous kerb, shared surface, planting and furniture as natural speed brakes — the street becomes an outdoor room that happens to admit vehicles.

The woonerf is the grandparent of the project's hub surface. The [[Ground Field]] with its [[Raised Narrowed Carriageway]] is a woonerf condition in miniature: one material plane, car movement permitted but visibly subordinated, dwelling activity (benches, play, planting) occupying the centre of gravity. Its history — codified in Dutch law in 1976 and exported worldwide as shared space — is expanded in [[History of the Woonerf]].
