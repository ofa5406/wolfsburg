# Electric Mobility

Battery-electric propulsion across the fleet — every vehicle in the project's system is electric, which turns hubs into pieces of energy infrastructure as much as mobility infrastructure. Charging points, solar canopies and grid connections appear throughout the [[Hub Toolpalette]]; the L tier concentrates high-power fleet charging ([[EV Fleet Charging at Scale]]), while S and M tiers host trickle charging for e-bikes and pods.

Electrification raises real questions the project logs rather than waves away: aggregate energy demand, grid capacity at peak charging windows (ideally anti-cyclical to the [[VW Shift Wave]]), and battery logistics. The future-projections research sketches demand curves to 2035/2050. Symbolically, electric mobility also matters in Wolfsburg: the city whose fortunes rose with the combustion engine hosting Germany's most visible post-combustion mobility system is the narrative arc of the whole proposal.
