# Intermodal Transfer

The act of changing modes within one journey — bike to bus, pod to e-bike, car to shuttle — and the single most fragile moment in any multimodal system. Every transfer costs time, cognitive load and comfort; research on transfer penalties shows travellers perceive waiting and orientation time as far longer than in-vehicle time. A system that ignores this loses to the private car's door-to-door continuity.

Hub design is largely transfer design. Short, legible walking lines between docks; real-time information at eye height; weather protection; level surfaces — the [[Hub Toolpalette]] is a catalogue of transfer-friction removers. The [[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]] is a choreography of the transfer moment, and [[Wayfinding]] elements make the next leg visible from the previous one. Anna's journey to the factory gate ([[Anna]]) is the project's worked example of transfers done well.
