# MaaS Levels

The widely used integration ladder for Mobility as a Service, from the Sochor et al. typology: **Level 0** — no integration, separate services; **Level 1** — integration of information (multimodal journey planners); **Level 2** — integration of booking and payment (one transaction across modes); **Level 3** — integration of the service offer (bundles, subscriptions, one account); **Level 4** — integration of societal goals (the platform steers behaviour toward public objectives like mode shift and emission cuts).

The ladder gives the project a precise ambition statement. A city of disconnected apps sits at Level 1; the [[Hub City]] presumes at least Level 3, one account across the whole [[Fleet]]; and the public ownership of the system implies Level 4 — the platform is tuned to maximise [[Modal Shift]] and hub coverage, not private revenue. Naming the level defends the proposal against "it's just an app" critiques.
