# Modal Shift

The movement of trips from one transport mode to another — in this project, from the private car to walking, cycling, public transport and the shared fleet. Modal shift is the *behavioural* outcome the whole spatial system is built to produce; the hubs, zones and fleet are supply, and the shift is demand responding.

The behaviour-change literature is clear that shift is won by **simultaneous push and pull** ([[Push and Pull Measures]]): making driving slower, costlier or less direct while making alternatives faster, cheaper and more dignified. Neither works alone. Timing matters too — people re-decide habits at [[Moments of Change]] like moving house or changing jobs, which is why the [[Implementation Timeline]] pairs each infrastructure phase with visible service improvements, and why the [[VW Shift Wave]] commute is targeted first: one habitual trip, made by thousands, on a predictable clock.
