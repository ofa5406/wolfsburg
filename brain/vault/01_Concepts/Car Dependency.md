# Car Dependency

The condition in which urban form, land use and daily routines make the private car practically mandatory rather than chosen. Dispersed destinations, parking-rich development, severed walking networks and thin transit create a loop: because everyone drives, the city is built for driving, which forces everyone to drive.

[[Donald Shoup]] identified minimum parking requirements as a key mechanism — [[Parking Minimums]] bundle car storage into every building, subsidising driving invisibly. [[Induced Demand]] explains the road-supply side of the same loop. Wolfsburg is an extreme case: a city co-designed with a car manufacturer, where the commute to the [[Volkswagen]] plant structured the entire settlement pattern. Breaking the loop therefore requires simultaneous moves on land use (hubs), network design ([[Filtered Permeability]]) and behaviour ([[Push and Pull Measures]]).
