# Superblock

The Barcelona model (*superilla*): take a group of city blocks — classically 3×3 — route through traffic around the perimeter, and convert the interior streets to walking, cycling, play and greenery, with vehicle access at walking pace for residents and deliveries only. The result is filtered permeability expressed as urban form: the city re-chunked into car-quiet cells.

Salvador Rueda's Barcelona programme is the flagship ([[Barcelona Superblocks]]); Vienna's Supergrätzl adaptation shows the model travels ([[Vienna Supergrätzl]]). The project's [[Five-Zone Model]] is a superblock logic scaled up to city sectors: zones with restricted entrances and calm interiors, boundaries carrying the through movement. The difference is the mobility offer inside — where Barcelona's interiors rely on walking and transit at the perimeter, Wolfsburg's zone interiors are seeded with S- and M-hubs so shared mobility penetrates the calm fabric.
