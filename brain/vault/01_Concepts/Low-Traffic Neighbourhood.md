# Low-Traffic Neighbourhood

The applied form of [[Filtered Permeability]] at neighbourhood scale, known by its London abbreviation LTN. Around a hundred schemes were implemented in London by 2022, using modal filters to remove through traffic from residential grids. The Aldred & Goodman "People and Places" survey associates LTNs with lower car use and more active travel for residents inside them, with displacement onto boundary roads smaller and more temporary than critics predicted ([[LTN Active Travel Evidence]]).

LTNs are also politically contested — studies document polarised community responses and equity questions about who benefits. The project treats that honestly: the boards acknowledge boundary-road and access concerns rather than claiming consensus, which is part of the [[Risk Register]] strategy of pre-empting the strongest objections instead of hiding from them.
