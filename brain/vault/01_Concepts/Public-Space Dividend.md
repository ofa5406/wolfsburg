# Public-Space Dividend

The project's name for the quantifiable gain in public space produced by pooling mobility. Every parking space dissolved into the hub network returns roughly 61 m² of land ([[61 Square Metres per Parking Space]]); every shared vehicle removes around sixteen private ones ([[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]]). Multiply the two and each hub becomes a small land-release engine.

Framing the gain as a *dividend* does rhetorical work: it presents reclaimed space as a return on an investment the city has already made, rather than a confiscation. The dividend is spent on the programme listed under [[Reclaimed Land]] — greenery, housing, play, canal-side parkland — and it is what the [[Street Transformation Sections]] draw in before/after form. It is the number that turns the thesis from ideology into accounting.
