# Adaptive Reuse

Converting existing buildings to new uses instead of demolishing them — preserving embodied carbon, structure and urban memory while replacing obsolete programme. Multi-storey car parks are prime candidates: generous spans, robust concrete frames, ramp access, and central locations, orphaned by declining car storage demand. Newcastle's "Karparc" conversion is a documented precedent ([[Newcastle Karparc]]).

Adaptive reuse is the architectural strategy of the entire L tier: all six [[L-Hub]] sites are existing multi-storey car parks. The [[L-Anchor]] variant converts the building into gastronomy, shops, community space and housing with fleet functions at the perimeter; the [[L-Gateway]] variant keeps more of the structure operational as depot and park-and-switch interchange. Reusing the Parking City's own monuments as the Hub City's anchors is the project's neatest symbolic move.
