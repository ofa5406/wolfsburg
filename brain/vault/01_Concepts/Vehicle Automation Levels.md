# Vehicle Automation Levels

The SAE J3016 scale, the standard vocabulary for what "autonomous" actually claims. **Level 0–2**: the human drives, with increasing assistance (cruise control, lane keeping). **Level 3**: conditional automation — the system drives in defined conditions but a human must take over on request. **Level 4**: high automation — no human needed within a bounded operational design domain (a geofenced district, mapped routes, weather limits). **Level 5**: full automation anywhere, which remains hypothetical.

The project's fleet assumes **Level 4 within a designed domain** — and that is a spatial claim, not just a technical one. The [[Five-Zone Model]]'s filtered, low-speed streets *are* an operational design domain: simplified traffic, restricted entries and predictable geometry make Level 4 tractable years earlier than open-road autonomy. The city is designed to meet the technology halfway.
