# Reclaimed Land

The payoff of the thesis: the land released when dispersed parking and oversized carriageways are no longer needed. The project treats this not as leftover space but as **the city's largest reserve of public land — already publicly owned, already paid for, currently under the cars.**

In Wolfsburg the reclaimed programme includes wider footpaths and protected cycling lanes, trees and climate infrastructure, pocket parks and public gardens, active ground floors at hub locations, canal-side parking converted to parkland, and new housing and community space. The arithmetic that quantifies it combines [[61 Square Metres per Parking Space]] with [[Bremen 16-to-1 Substitution]]: a hub pooling twenty shared vehicles can stand in for roughly 320 private cars — on the order of 19,500 m² of parking that converts to streets, gardens and squares.
