# Walking Catchment

The area from which people will actually walk to a facility — the honest unit of coverage for any hub network. Catchments are behavioural, not geometric: acceptable walking distance grows with the quality of the destination and the walk itself, which is why the tiers carry different radii — 150–250 m for an [[S-Hub]], 300–500 m for an [[M-Hub]], 700–1000 m for an [[L-Hub]].

Nesting these catchments produces the coverage logic of the [[Hub System]]: small hubs fill the gaps between medium ones, medium between large, so the whole populated fabric sits within a short walk of *some* access point. The distinction between walking catchments and service ranges matters: the web tool's early coverage radii (500/2000/4000 m) describe vehicle service areas, not walks — a documented mismatch tracked as [[Coverage Radius Mismatch]].
