# Ridepooling

Combining multiple passengers with compatible origins, destinations and timings into one vehicle and one composed route — the mechanism that gives shared mobility its capacity advantage. A pooled shuttle moving six passengers replaces six car trips with one vehicle movement; multiplied across a city, pooling is what lets 763 vehicles do the work of tens of thousands of private cars.

Pooling has a comfort price — detours and stops — so its success depends on demand density and honest service promises (maximum wait, maximum detour). Wolfsburg's peculiar advantage is the [[VW Shift Wave]]: thousands of workers moving between the same origins and one destination on a fixed clock is the *easiest possible pooling problem*, the opposite of the dispersed demand that makes ridepooling hard elsewhere. [[MOIA]] in Hamburg demonstrates the model commercially, in VW's own corporate family.
