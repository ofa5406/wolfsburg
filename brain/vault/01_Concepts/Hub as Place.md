# Hub as Place

One of the two natures of the [[Mobility Hub]]: the hub as a spatial public-life anchor. In the design concept a hub is recognisable before any vehicle appears — a reddish stone [[Ground Field]] claims the surface, a [[Canopy Beacon]] marks it vertically, the [[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]] choreographs movement from vehicle edge through threshold to dwelling edge, and a palette of seating, greenery, play and cultural elements makes it somewhere to *be*, not just pass through.

This reading is what separates the project from a fleet-operations diagram. The group's June 2026 debate ("these are garages, not hubs") was precisely a defence of hub-as-place against a purely operational reading, resolved by protecting the surface typology wherever the location logic is parking-driven ([[Hub System Rethink]]).
