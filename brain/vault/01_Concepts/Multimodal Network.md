# Multimodal Network

A transport network in which several modes — walking, cycling, buses, shuttles, shared cars, micro-pods — operate as complementary layers rather than competitors, each covering the trip lengths and purposes it serves best. The art is in the seams: a multimodal network is only as good as its transfer points, which is where the [[Mobility Hub]] earns its centrality.

The project's network assigns roles deliberately: walking carries sub-kilometre trips within hub catchments; e-bikes and the [[Rad Network]] carry 1–5 km; autonomous buses and shuttle pods carry trunk flows including the [[VW Shift Wave]]; micro-pods and shared EVs cover irregular, encumbered or off-peak trips. The [[Five-Zone Model]] protects the slow layers from through traffic, and [[Mobility as a Service]] binds the layers into one legible service.
