# Micromobility

The family of small, light, low-speed vehicles — bikes, e-bikes, e-scooters, cargo bikes, and in this project's extension, autonomous micro-pods — that carry one or two people over short urban distances. Micromobility's spatial efficiency is its superpower: a dozen e-bikes fit in one car parking space, and a micro-pod needs a fraction of a car's road width and none of its storage footprint.

The project leans on micromobility for the trips that decide the system's fairness: the last few hundred metres, the shopping run, the mobility-impaired user's dock-to-door leg ([[Adaptive Mobility Dock]]). Micromobility is also the tier-defining service of the [[S-Hub]] — the smallest hubs are essentially micromobility points — and the reason the reclaimed street sections can be so generous: the modes that replace the car simply need less asphalt.
