# Personas Index

The lives the system must serve — the equity table with names.

**The proof journey:** [[Anna]] — VW worker, home to factory gate without a private car; the integration test of the whole system, run through the [[VW Shift Wave]].

**The exhibition cycle:** [[Thomas]] (44, builds the fleet and rides it) · [[Sabine]] (38, two kids, two cars dissolved into one shuttle) · [[Lukas]] (13, the city becomes his) · [[Gertrude]] (85, market on Tuesdays again).

Each persona is engineered against a specific failure mode named in [[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]]: the shift worker's night commute ([[Shift Workers Face Double Commutes]]), the carer's trip chain ([[Trip Chaining]]), the teenager's dependence, the mobility-impaired resident's exclusion. Together they are the project's answer to "who is this for?" — and its shield against the war-on-the-worker story ([[Risk — Company Town Politics]]).
