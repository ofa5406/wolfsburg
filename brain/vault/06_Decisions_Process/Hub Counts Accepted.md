# Hub Counts Accepted

The network's shape, locked June 11, 2026: **6 L-hubs, 19 M-hubs, 43 S-hubs — 68 hubs total.** The six L are the existing multi-storey car parks ([[L-Hubs from Car Parks Decision]]); the nineteen M are manually designed; the forty-three S are placed algorithmically ([[Grasshopper S-Hub Placement]]). A higher M count was discussed and rejected for manageability.

The counts encode the coverage argument: nested walking radii (L 700–1000 m, M 300–500 m, S 150–250 m) tiling the centre so that most residents live within a short walk of some hub — the claim the [[Hub Coverage Map]] must demonstrate at 80–90% within 300 m ([[800 Metre Catchment Standard]], [[Hub Tier System]]).

Sixty-eight is also a rhetorical number: large enough to read as a system rather than a pilot, small enough to be believable as a first-phase build ([[Implementation Timeline]]). The later hub-system debate stress-tested what the tiers *mean* but never reopened the counts ([[Hub System Rethink]]).
