# Prove the System Decision

The decision that redirected the whole project (June 4, 2026): **stop inventing vehicle types; prove the hub-based system works spatially and operationally.** The reviewer's verdict — "move beyond compelling images to prove the system actually works" — accepted the vision and changed the task: from imagining the post-car city to demonstrating it.

Everything after this date is shaped by it. The [[Proof Framework]] defines what counts as demonstration; the fleet arithmetic ([[Fleet Sizing]]), coverage analysis ([[Hub Coverage Map]]) and persona journeys exist because assertion stopped being enough. The companion decision from the same session locked the L-hubs to the six existing multi-storey car parks ([[L-Hubs from Car Parks Decision]]).

The decision also explains a discipline visible throughout the brain: every claim carries a number, every number a source, every source a confidence flag. The project's epistemics were set by a tutor's single sentence — and the knowledge graph you are reading is, in a sense, that sentence executed completely ([[Urban Proof]]).
