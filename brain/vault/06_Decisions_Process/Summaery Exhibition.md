# Summaery Exhibition

The public showing: **Summaery at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, July 9–12, 2026** — the university's annual exhibition, where `<stadt.hub>` runs unattended on a kiosk screen in the computer pool. No narrator, no pitch: visitors encounter the project alone, which makes the exhibition the hardest communication test the work faces — everything must explain itself ([[System Identity]]).

The constraint shaped a family of artefacts: the self-running exhibition deck (typed headlines, embedded live maps, the hub-viewer camera tour, idle-detection that resumes the loop when visitors walk away), the interactive [[Hub Viewer]], and this knowledge graph itself — the project's brain, made explorable so a visitor can wander the reasoning the way they wander the drawings ([[Wolfsburg Activity Map]]).

Summaery sits between the jury dates: after the [[June 25 Studio Final]] proved the system to tutors, the exhibition proves it to civilians — and what survives both goes to the [[Wolfsburg Award Competition]] in August.
