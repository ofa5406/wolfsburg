# Fleet Sizing Formula Accepted

The quantitative foundation cleared its first review (June 11, 2026): tutors **accepted the fleet-sizing logic** — 100,000 trips/day derived from the three-block demand basis (17,000 residents, 18,000 workers, 17,000 visitors), a ~9,000-trip peak hour, and the division into modes by trip length and purpose ([[Fleet Sizing]], [[Demand Basis]], [[Modal Distribution Model]]).

The same consultation locked the **fleet composition**: 131 e-bikes (fixed docks chosen over free-floating for predictability), 55 autonomous shuttle pods, 33 autonomous buses, 369 micro-pods, 175 shared EVs — 763 vehicles ([[Fleet]]).

Acceptance came with an implicit debt the risk register tracks: the formula is a first-order analytical estimate, not an agent-based simulation, and a sharp jury may ask "did you simulate or just divide?" ([[Methodology vs Code Gap]], [[VW Shift Wave Simulation]]). The decision's real product was permission to move from sizing the system to proving it ([[Proof Framework]]) — the "proof phase" the project has been in since.
