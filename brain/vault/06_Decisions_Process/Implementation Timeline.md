# Implementation Timeline

The four-phase transition from today's parking city to the post-car centre — the deliverable that makes the vision *dated* rather than utopian, and the container into which every phasing argument in the brain drains.

The phases follow the research's Seed/Shift/Post-car logic ([[AV Timelines Keep Slipping]]): **Phase 1 (≈2026–2030)** — driver-operated pooled shuttles, e-bikes and shared EVs prove the hub geometry now; first L-hub conversions; pilot parking-to-plaza projects. **Phase 2 (≈2030–2035)** — supervised Level-4 autonomy on the VW corridor, tied explicitly to MOIA's Hamburg timeline ([[ID Buzz AD Production Level 4]]); the full 68-hub network operational; most surface parking reclaimed. **Phase 3 (2035+)** — fleet-wide autonomy as economics, not precondition; land fully repurposed. A **Phase 0** runs beneath: narrative and coalition — VW, Works Council, IG Metall as co-authors ([[NEW AUTO Strategy]]).

The sequencing rules are evidence, not taste: pull before push ([[Push Beats Pull Alone]]), trials before permanence ([[Stockholm Congestion Charge]]), launch into the disruption window ([[Golf Moves to Puebla]]), and never let a slipped AV date strand the plan — the city works on day one with drivers ([[Phased Autonomy]]).
