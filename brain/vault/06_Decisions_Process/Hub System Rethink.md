# Hub System Rethink

The project's most consequential internal debate (June 17, 2026). Trigger: the web tool places hubs from existing parking data — M-hubs at underground car parks, L-hubs at multi-storey ones — which made the hubs read as *garages*, and one position argued surface hubs are pointless if autonomous vehicles are everywhere. The debate reopened the hub concept itself; the analysis lives in `project/hub_concept_vs_tool.md`.

The resolution protected the typology by separating two questions the debate had fused: **location** (parking infrastructure may determine *where* a hub sits, for fast vehicle servicing) and **place** (the hub remains the surface public space the typology defines). S-hubs unchanged — their placement was never parking-driven. M-hubs became **parking-served places**: residual parking absorbed underground, full surface typology kept. L split into two sub-types: the central **L-Anchor** (car-park building as neighbourhood anchor) and the edge **L-Gateway** (fleet depot + park-and-switch interchange) ([[L-Anchor]], [[L-Gateway]], [[L-Hub Anchor vs Depot]]).

The rejected position mattered: abandoning surface hubs would reduce the project to mobility infrastructure and discard the public-life thesis ([[Hub as Place]], [[Catalyst Not Shelter]]). Implementation gaps stayed open and tracked ([[Coverage Radius Mismatch]], [[Methodology vs Code Gap]]).
