# Car-Land Composite Map

The project's rhetorical anchor: a single composite visualisation of **all land currently dedicated to cars** in central Wolfsburg — surface parking, multi-storey garages, on-street bays, and the car-oriented share of the street sections — drawn as one continuous figure. Not an analysis map; an *indictment* map.

Its power is aggregation. Each lot and lane reads as normal in isolation; unioned into one shape, the car's land claim becomes a landscape — and in the city with Germany's highest car density, that shape is expected to rival the parks and public spaces combined ([[Wolfsburg Highest Car Density]], [[Car-Dedicated Land]]).

The map is also the project's budget sheet: everything inside the shape is the raw material of the transformation — hub sites, housing plots, plazas, the [[Public-Space Dividend]] made cartographic ([[Reclaimed Land]]). The masterplan layers exist in Rhino; the export is the remaining task ([[Rhino Toolpalette Kit]]). On the boards it opens the argument the way Shoup opens the theory: look at what the parking city actually costs ([[61 Square Metres per Parking Space]]).
