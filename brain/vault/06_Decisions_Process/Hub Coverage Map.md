# Hub Coverage Map

The demonstration that the network reaches everyone: all **68 hubs mapped with their walking catchments** — isochrones around every S, M and L location — proving the coverage claim of **80–90% of residents within 300 metres of an S- or M-hub** ([[Hub Counts Accepted]], [[Walking Catchment]]).

The map does double duty in the argument. As system proof, it shows the nested radii actually tile the centre — the fine S-grain filling the gaps between M anchors, the whole staying inside the international 800 m standard while over-delivering beneath it ([[800 Metre Catchment Standard]], [[Service Radius]]). As equity proof, it answers the hardest fairness question with geometry: the resident who cannot cycle, the parent with a pram, the night-shift worker — coverage is what guarantees the system serves them, so the walking-catchment map is also the equity map ([[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]], [[Shift Workers Face Double Commutes]]).

The geometry exists in the Rhino masterplan; export remains open. Its honest footnote: the web tool's coverage radii differ from the typology's walking catchments, a tracked inconsistency ([[Coverage Radius Mismatch]]).
