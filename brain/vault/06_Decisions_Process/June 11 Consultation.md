# June 11 Consultation

The most productive hour of the project: the **June 11, 2026 tutor consultation** locked five decisions in a single session — the fleet sizing formula and composition ([[Fleet Sizing Formula Accepted]]), the hub counts ([[Hub Counts Accepted]]), the five-zone Groningen structure ([[Zone Structure Decision]]), algorithmic S-hub placement ([[Grasshopper S-Hub Placement]]), and the VW-gate case study ([[VW Gate Case Study Decision]]).

The session marks the project's phase change. Before it, the quantitative foundation was proposal; after it, the foundation was accepted and the task became proof — demonstrating coverage, capacity and experience on top of numbers the tutors had signed off ([[Proof Framework]]).

Methodologically it models the project's consultation discipline: come with decisions framed as accept/reject choices with alternatives named, leave with a written record in `decisions.md`, and never reopen what was locked without a documented reason — the discipline that later contained the hub-system debate to the one question it actually raised ([[Hub System Rethink]]).
