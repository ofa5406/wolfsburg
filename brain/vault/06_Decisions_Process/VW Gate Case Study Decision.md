# VW Gate Case Study Decision

Locked June 11, 2026: **the hub adjacent to the Volkswagen factory campus is the primary detailed case study.** A city-centre hub may follow as a secondary example, but the design deep-dive, the before/after drawings and the persona journey all anchor at the factory gate ([[VW Factory Gate]]).

The logic is maximum-stress proof: if the system demonstrably handles the hardest case — the [[VW Shift Wave]], ~10,000 workers through one interchange in about an hour — every gentler condition follows *a fortiori*. And the hardest case is simultaneously the most favourable one, because the wave is predictable, concentrated and reversible ([[Commuter Corridor]]) — the case study lets the project show both facts at once.

It is also the narrative's strongest image: the gate where the parking city was born daily, remade as the place where the post-car city boards ([[Post-Car City in VW's Hometown]]). Anna's journey ends here ([[Anna]]); the L-hub drawings, the three-zone arrival sequence and the capacity table all render this one location ([[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]], [[Capacity Analysis]]).
