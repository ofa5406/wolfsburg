# Street Transformation Sections

The deliverable that makes the transformation *legible at eye level*: **before/after street sections for Kleiststraße and at least one other street**, drawn metre by metre — parking lane by parking lane — showing exactly which strip of asphalt becomes pavement, cycle track, trees, seating or an S-hub footprint ([[Kleiststraße]], [[Street-Space Reallocation]]).

Sections carry the argument maps cannot: a plan shows *that* space is reclaimed; a section shows *what it feels like to stand in it*. The before drawing states the diagnosis (carriageway plus parked cars consuming the width between façades); the after drawing spends the dividend — the kerb lane's ~20% of street space converted first, since it needs only paint and bollards ([[On-Street Parking 20 Percent of Kerb Space]]).

The sections also anchor the honesty of the phasing: each after-state is dated to a phase of the [[Implementation Timeline]], so the drawing set reads as a construction sequence, not a rendering of paradise ([[Tactical Urbanism]]). NACTO's street-design vocabulary supplies the dimensional standards the redrawn sections cite ([[Street as Public Space]]).
