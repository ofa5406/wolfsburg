# Proof Framework

The project's definition of done — the six demonstrations that must exist before the final submission, set after the tutors demanded proof over imagery ([[Prove the System Decision]]):

1. **Hub coverage** — all 68 hubs mapped with walking catchments; 80–90% of residents within 300 m of an S- or M-hub ([[Hub Coverage Map]]).
2. **Fleet sizing logic** — the calculation showing the fleet clears a VW shift wave of ~10,000 workers in about an hour ([[Capacity Analysis]], [[Shift Wave Capacity Check]]).
3. **Persona journey** — one detailed home-to-factory-gate trip without a private car: time, transfers, experience ([[Anna]]).
4. **Car-land map** — the single composite of all land currently dedicated to cars; the project's rhetorical anchor ([[Car-Land Composite Map]]).
5. **Street transformation sections** — before/after for Kleiststraße and at least one other street ([[Street Transformation Sections]]).
6. **Implementation timeline** — the four-phase transition from today to the post-car centre ([[Implementation Timeline]]).

Each item answers a specific objection from the risk register; together they convert the vision into a checkable system ([[Risk Register]], [[Urban Proof]]).
