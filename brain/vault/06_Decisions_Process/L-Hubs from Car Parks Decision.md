# L-Hubs from Car Parks Decision

Locked June 4, 2026: **the six L-hubs are the six existing central multi-storey car parks, repurposed.** New purpose-built anchors were considered and rejected — the existing structures are more spatially and narratively convincing.

The pragmatics: the frames are already built, centrally located, structurally sized for heavy use, and owned by the parking economy the project dissolves — tens of millions of euros of sunk concrete converted rather than demolished ([[Adaptive Reuse]], [[Parking Space Costs 20 to 35 Thousand]]). Karparc and the wider conversion wave prove the market consents to exactly this move ([[Newcastle Karparc]]).

The symbolism does equal work: the building type that *is* the parking city becomes the anchor of the hub city — the thesis performed in concrete ([[From Parking City to Hub City]]).

The June 17 rethink refined the decision without reversing it: central conversions became **L-Anchors** (mostly non-parking public programme), while edge car parks gained a second sub-type, the **L-Gateway** depot-interchange ([[Hub System Rethink]], [[L-Anchor]], [[L-Gateway]]).
