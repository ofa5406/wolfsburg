# June 25 Studio Final

The first of the project's three deadlines: the **studio final presentation at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, June 25, 2026** — the review for which the entire proof phase was assembled. The tutors' June 4 demand ("prove the system works") set the agenda ([[Prove the System Decision]]); the deliverable list was the [[Proof Framework]] executed: hub coverage map, fleet-sizing appendix, persona journey, car-land composite, street sections, implementation timeline.

The presentation form it produced — the scroll deck with live embedded maps, later rebuilt for the exhibition — established the project's visual identity: near-monochrome, typed headlines, real interactive components instead of screenshots ([[System Identity]]).

In the project's timeline the studio final is the hinge between *making the argument* and *exhibiting it*: what survived the jury went forward to the [[Summaery Exhibition]] (July 9–12) and the [[Wolfsburg Award Competition]] (August 16), and the risk register was written explicitly as preparation for this room's hardest questions ([[Risk Register]]).
