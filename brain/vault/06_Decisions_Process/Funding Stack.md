# Funding Stack

The answer to the standard jury kill-shot — *"beautiful, but who pays?"* — assembled from working models rather than optimism. Three layers stack:

**City funds the shells.** Hub sites and public realm come from the freed land itself plus EU/federal sustainable-mobility grants — the land is already public, which is the hidden budget ([[Reclaimed Land]], [[Public-Space Dividend]]). **Operators fund the fleets.** Berlin's Jelbi and Vancouver's Mobi run as city-plus-operator partnerships: the city provides sites, operators finance and run their own vehicles — and VW/MOIA's turnkey licensing model removes the fleet-capex risk entirely ([[MOIA]], [[NEW AUTO Strategy]]). **Land value closes the loop.** Hong Kong MTR's rail-plus-property model runs profitably without subsidy by capturing value around stations; Wolfsburg's hubs sit on exactly such appreciating freed land, and L-Anchor housing and ground-floor programme monetise it ([[L-Anchor]], [[Surface Lot to Dozens of Homes]]).

The honest tension stays on the page: operators want the public sector to carry risk; value capture is the mechanism that answers it. Nottingham shows the transitional revenue instrument ([[Nottingham Workplace Parking Levy]]).
