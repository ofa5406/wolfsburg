# Zone Structure Decision

Locked June 11, 2026: the city centre divides into **five zones on the Groningen model** — filtered permeability, one-way internal streets, restricted zone entrances that prevent through-traffic while keeping every address reachable ([[Five-Zone Model]], [[Groningen Traffic Circulation Plan]]).

Two alternatives were weighed and rejected. A **full car-exclusion zone** was judged too radical for the proof stage — it would shift the argument from system design to prohibition politics, and the evidence says it is unnecessary ([[25 Percent Traffic Cut Saves 200 Lives]]). The **Barcelona superblock model** was considered and set aside as less directly applicable: superblocks tile a dense grid; Groningen's sector logic fits a centre organised around a ring, which is Wolfsburg's actual geometry ([[Barcelona Superblocks]]).

Tutors were receptive, and the decision has held through every later debate. Its operational edge — where inbound private cars are intercepted — was refined later into the L-Gateway sub-type ([[Hub System Rethink]], [[L-Gateway]]).
