# 15-Minute City Conspiracy

The strangest opposition the lineage has faced — recorded because the exhibition may meet it. From late 2022, conspiracy theories reframed the 15-minute city as a plot to **confine residents to zones**, fusing it with Oxford's unrelated traffic-filter trial and pandemic-era lockdown anxieties; the claim spread through international networks and reached parliaments.

The factual rebuttal is one sentence: proximity planning **adds destinations near people; it never restricts movement** — the 15 minutes measure what you can *reach*, not where you may *go* ([[15-Minute City]], [[Proximity Planning]]). Nothing in Moreno's framework, or in any implementation, gates travel; the confusion deliberately conflates access planning with traffic filters, which themselves filter only *through-driving*, never persons ([[Modal Filter]], [[Freight and Emergency Access]]).

The strategic lesson the project takes: name the myth once, clearly, on the boards — unanswered, it fills the vacuum; over-answered, it steals the frame ([[Imposition Without Consent]], [[Carlos Moreno]]). Access is the whole point; confinement is its opposite.
