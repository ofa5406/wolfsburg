# Planting Bed and Bioswale

A linear planted bed that can double as a stormwater channel, placed at the [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]] or between zones — mandatory from the M tier. The double function is the point: the same strip is greenery to the eye and drainage to the storm, catching runoff from the stone field and letting it infiltrate instead of loading the sewers.

Bioswales convert the hub from impervious problem to sponge-city component, working with the [[Permeable Surface Zone]] as the hub's hydrological pair. The bed also draws the zone boundary physically — a soft green line where the palette's diagram draws an abstract one — demonstrating the typology's habit of making structure out of useful things rather than markings.
