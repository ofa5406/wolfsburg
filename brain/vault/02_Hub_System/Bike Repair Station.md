# Bike Repair Station

Self-service tools and a pump, integrated at the [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]] edge, mandatory from the M tier. The station keeps the cycling layer honest: a network that runs on bikes needs bike care to be as ambient as the bikes themselves, and a flat tyre five hundred metres from home should be a five-minute pause, not a reason to want a car back.

The element also carries a maintenance-culture message: the system trusts users with tools. Together with the [[Tool Equipment Lending]] option and the [[Cargo Bike Lending Library]], it sketches the hub as a place of shared equipment rather than pure consumption — a quiet extension of [[Access over Ownership]] from vehicles to the things that keep vehicles running.
