# Luggage Storage

Short-term storage near factory gates, train connections and major interchange points — the element for journeys that carry more than a person. The commuter's gym bag, the traveller's suitcase between train and pod, the worker's equipment across a shift: each is a small argument for a private boot, and the locker answers it at the interchange instead.

Deployment maps onto the system's heavy seams: the [[VW Factory Gate]] case-study hub, rail-station adjacencies, and the [[Park-and-Switch Interchange]] where arriving drivers must trust the system with their cargo as well as themselves. Like the parcel lockers it borrows a proven station technology and distributes it to the network's pressure points.
