# Gastronomy and Permanent Programme

The L-Anchor's defining content: café, restaurant, shops, cultural and community spaces occupying the transformed ground floor — **permanent or semi-permanent programme, explicitly not vendors**. In Scenario A the existing car-park structure is retained and its ground floor rebuilt around this programme; in Scenario B pavilions each carry one programme piece around a shared open space.

The distinction from the kiosk slot is categorical: a vendor activates a corner, a restaurant anchors a neighbourhood evening. Permanent programme is what makes the [[L-Anchor]] a destination people visit with no journey in mind — the mobility system underwriting civic space with its real estate. Upper floors opened to housing, studios and events complete the conversion from single-use storage to mixed-use anchor ([[Mixed Use]]).
