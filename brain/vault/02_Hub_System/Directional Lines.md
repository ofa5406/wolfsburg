# Directional Lines

Lines embedded in the reddish stone field, pointing toward each mobility element type — **part of the material, not a separate sign**. Mandatory at every tier. They begin at the hub's edges and the threshold, fan out to the e-bike rack, the micro-pod zone, the EV bay, and continue across the carriageway where the field crosses the street, guiding the crossing itself.

The lines are the purest expression of the [[Smooth Usage]] principle: navigation you walk on rather than read. They also carry the system's graphic identity into the ground plane — the same line language at all 68 hubs means a user who has learned one hub has learned them all, which is [[System Identity]] doing wayfinding work.
