# Toolpalette Category — Environment

The ecological layer: [[Tree with Stone Grate]], [[Planting Bed and Bioswale]], and [[Permeable Surface Zone]], extended by the optional [[Wildflower Meadow Strip]] and [[Living Wall]]. Every hub is required to carry environmental generosity — it is written into the [[Catalyst Not Shelter]] principle — so trees and planting are palette elements with per-tier requirements, not landscaping added later.

The category also does climate-infrastructure work: bioswales channel stormwater, permeable paving infiltrates it, tree canopy shades the waiting zones and cools the stone field. Sixty-eight hubs distributed across the city amount to a modest but real green network, laid precisely where asphalt used to be — each hub a small demonstration that the space cars vacate can literally soak up rain and cast shade.
