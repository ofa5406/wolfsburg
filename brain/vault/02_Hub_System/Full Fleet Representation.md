# Full Fleet Representation

The L-tier requirement that **all five mobility modes are present**: autonomous bus, shuttle pod, micro-pod, shared EV and e-cargo bike, each with its docking, boarding and charging interface. Mandatory at every [[L-Hub]]. The requirement makes the L-hub the system's complete showroom — the one place where the entire mobility offer is visible, comparable and boardable side by side.

Completeness serves both users and the argument. Users get guaranteed availability of *some* suitable mode at the anchor, whatever the trip; the project gets its proof-of-system stage — a single photograph of an L-hub forecourt shows the whole 763-vehicle ecology in one frame, which is why the case-study hub at the [[VW Factory Gate]] carries this element so prominently.
