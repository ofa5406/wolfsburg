# Hub System

The project's central design object: a network of **68 mobility hubs** across central Wolfsburg — 6 [[L-Hub]], 19 [[M-Hub]] and 43 [[S-Hub]] — replacing dispersed private parking as the organising element of urban mobility. The tiers nest by walking catchment so that 80–90% of residents live within 300 m of an S- or M-hub ([[Hub Coverage]]), and every hub shares one design language: the reddish stone [[Ground Field]], a [[Vertical Beacon]], the [[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]], and elements composed from the [[Hub Toolpalette]].

The system is simultaneously infrastructure (docking, charging, dispatch for the 763-vehicle [[Fleet]]) and place (public-life anchors in the street). Its network logic follows the [[Hub Tier System]]; its placement logic combines algorithmic analysis with design judgement; its flagship case study is the hub at the [[VW Factory Gate]].
