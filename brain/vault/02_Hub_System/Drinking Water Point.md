# Drinking Water Point

Public water access in [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]], mandatory from the M tier. A small element with a long civic pedigree — the public fountain has marked genuinely public space since antiquity, and its modern scarcity tracks the privatisation of the street. Restoring it at hubs signals that these are places to inhabit, not just transit.

Practically it serves the system's active users: cyclists refilling on the Rad Network, children at the play element, anyone in a heat wave — hub trees plus water points make the network a modest climate-adaptation grid. Like every palette element it is integrated into the hub's furniture language rather than standing as a generic catalogue item.
