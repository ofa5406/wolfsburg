# Bench

The linear bench for individual users in [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]] — minimum one mandatory at every tier, including the smallest S-hub. Its mandate is a design-political statement disguised as furniture: even the most minimal hub must offer a place to sit that asks nothing in return, because sitting is how a piece of mobility infrastructure first behaves like public space.

The bench is also the palette's cheapest [[Public Life]] generator — the element that serves the resident who is not travelling at all, the waiting grandparent, the pause in a walk. Variants extend it: [[Group Seating]] for the social version at M and L, [[Personal Device Charging]] embedded where dwell times are long, heating in the enclosed waiting zones.
