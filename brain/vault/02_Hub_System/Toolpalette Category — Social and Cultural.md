# Toolpalette Category — Social and Cultural

The programme layer that turns an interchange into an anchor: the [[Community Information Board]], [[Vendor Kiosk Slot]], [[Art and Cultural Marker]], and [[Flexible Surface Area]], with the optional family adding play, games, book exchange, markets and memory. These elements occupy [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]] and the generous open spaces of L-hubs.

The category is the direct execution of [[Catalyst Not Shelter]]: mobility is the anchor, social life is the ambition. Each element seeds a different social rhythm — the kiosk a daily one, the market slot a weekly one, the art marker a permanent one — so the hub accumulates reasons to exist beyond travel. The one-per-hub art marker rule also gives each of the 68 hubs an individual identity inside the shared system language.
