# Micro-library Book Exchange

A book exchange integrated into the hub's structural element at high-dwell neighbourhood hubs. The give-one-take-one shelf is among the most successful micro-civic inventions of recent decades: nearly free, self-organising, and disproportionately loved — a piece of infrastructure that runs on trust and returns neighbourhood identity.

At a hub it converts waiting into browsing and strangers into a reading public; like the [[Community Information Board]] it needs exactly what a hub generates (daily passage, brief pauses, shelter) and gives back exactly what a hub needs (a reason to linger, a token of shared ownership). Its integration into the canopy or seating follows the palette's rule that nothing worthwhile stands as separate clutter.
