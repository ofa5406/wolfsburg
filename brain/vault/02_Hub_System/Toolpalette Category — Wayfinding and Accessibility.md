# Toolpalette Category — Wayfinding and Accessibility

The legibility layer of the palette: [[Directional Lines]] embedded in the stone field, [[Tactile Paving Strips]] at every transition, and the [[Hub Identity Marker]] that names each hub and maps its neighbours. The category is deliberately small and deliberately mandatory — all three elements are required at every tier, because a hub you cannot read is a hub you cannot use.

Its philosophy follows the [[Smooth Usage]] principle: wayfinding is built into the material of the hub rather than posted beside it. Accessibility is treated as the same problem, not a separate one — the tactile strips serve blind users exactly as the directional lines serve sighted ones, one surface carrying both channels. The result extends [[Wayfinding]] from signage discipline to ground-plane design.
