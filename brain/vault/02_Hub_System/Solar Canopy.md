# Solar Canopy

A canopy variant that generates energy where viable — and visibly, **as a design statement**. The photovoltaic roof closes a satisfying loop: the structure that shelters the e-bikes powers their charging, and the hub advertises its own energy story to everyone who looks up.

Honesty matters at this scale: a hub canopy's yield is modest against the fleet's total demand, so the element is framed as visible contribution and grid-softening trickle charge, not as the system's power plant — bulk energy lives at the L-tier's [[EV Fleet Charging at Scale]]. The visibility is the second function: in the city that built combustion engines, infrastructure that photosynthesises its own operation is an argument made of hardware ([[Electric Mobility]]).
