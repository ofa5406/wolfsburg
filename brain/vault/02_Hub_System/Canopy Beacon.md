# Canopy Beacon

The overhead structure at [[Zone 2 — Threshold]] — the hub's [[Vertical Beacon]] built as shelter. Mandatory at S and M tiers (the L-hub's building replaces it), it shelters the moment of waiting and orientation, may merge with the [[Public Transport Shed]] into a single unified roof, and carries the [[Ambient Responsive Lighting]] that makes the hub glow gently at night.

Its double duty is the design economy of the palette in miniature: one structure is simultaneously weather protection, fifty-metre landmark, lighting armature and — in the [[Solar Canopy]] variant — power plant. Material and form are defined per typology drawing rather than fixed, keeping room for the character study the tier sheets develop, but the role is invariant: the roof announces the system.
