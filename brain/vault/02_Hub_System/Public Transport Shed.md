# Public Transport Shed

Shelter at fixed bus or tram stops — present at any tier where a public transport route overlaps the hub, and **combinable with the canopy into one unified structure**. The merge rule matters architecturally: rather than a hub canopy and a bus shelter standing awkwardly side by side, the two become a single roof, and the hub absorbs the transit stop into its own identity.

The shed carries the waiting-dignity agenda for the highest-capacity mode: enclosed and heated variants at exposed locations follow the Zurich VBZ precedent ([[Zurich VBZ Tram Shelters]]). Because the S-hub placement logic grows from existing bus stops, the shed is also the element through which the old transit network is inherited and upgraded rather than replaced.
