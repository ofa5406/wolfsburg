# M-Hub

**"The Choice Point" — 19 hubs · neighbourhood scale · walking radius 300–500 m.** The tier where you have options: multiple modes, a short wait, probably a transfer. The design is a real place, not a pause — at an M-hub you can see live which vehicles are available before you decide, via the [[Real-time Info Terminal]].

Mandatory equipment is everything at S plus: [[Cargo Bike Dock]], [[Shared EV Bay]], [[AV Drop-off and Pickup Zone]], a larger canopy (or one combined with the [[Public Transport Shed]]), [[Group Seating]], [[Drinking Water Point]], [[Bike Repair Station]], [[Planting Bed and Bioswale]], [[Permeable Surface Zone]] and at least one [[Tree with Stone Grate]]. Location logic ties M-hubs to underground car parks — residual parking goes below, fast AV servicing stays close — while the full surface typology is protected above ([[Hub System Rethink]]). Ground field: 18–25 m by 25–35 m on a main neighbourhood street.
