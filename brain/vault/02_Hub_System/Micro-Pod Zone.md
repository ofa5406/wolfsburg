# Micro-Pod Zone

The marked area within the stone field where autonomous micro-pods stop — mandatory at every tier, with [[Directional Lines]] leading users to it. The zone is pavement markings and geometry rather than built structure: a pod-length of kerb kept clear, positioned so boarding never conflicts with the cycle docks beside it.

Its modesty is the point. The micro-pod ([[Micro-Pod]]) is the fleet's most numerous vehicle — 369 of them — and its stop must be replicable 68 times without heavy construction, which is why it lives as paint and paving inside [[Zone 1 — Vehicle Edge]]. At S-hubs the micro-pod zone plus the e-bike rack constitute the entire vehicle interface: the minimum viable hub.
