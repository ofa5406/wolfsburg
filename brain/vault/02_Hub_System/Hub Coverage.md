# Hub Coverage

The first demonstration in the [[Proof Framework]]: all 68 hubs mapped with their walking catchments, showing that **80–90% of residents are within 300 m of an S- or M-hub**. Coverage is the system's fairness claim — the promise that shared mobility is not a downtown amenity but a whole-fabric service — and the map that shows it is a core deliverable ([[Hub Coverage Map]]).

Methodologically the claim depends on how catchments are drawn: crow-flies radii flatter the network, network-distance isochrones respect severance and are honest. The known wrinkle is the [[Coverage Radius Mismatch]] — the web tool's service radii (500/2000/4000 m) must not be presented as walking coverage. The masterplan Rhino file carries the drawn catchment geometry; the coverage figure is the headline that must survive jury scrutiny.
