# Heated Waiting Zone

An enclosed or semi-enclosed seating area with heating, applied at cold or exposed locations — an optional element written for Wolfsburg winters. Northern German cold is a modal-shift force in its own right: every January morning a warm private car argues against the whole system, and the heated zone is the palette's direct rebuttal.

The precedent is Zurich's VBZ practice of fully enclosed, heated tram shelters ([[Zurich VBZ Tram Shelters]]), which treats transit waiting as an indoor activity. Deployment follows exposure and dwell time: factory-gate hubs with shift-change waits, windswept edge sites, interchanges where transfers are timed. The element upgrades the [[Public Transport Shed]] and canopy family from shelter to hospitality.
