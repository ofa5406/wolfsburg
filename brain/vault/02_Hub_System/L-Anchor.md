# L-Anchor

The central sub-type of the [[L-Hub]], created in the 2026-06-17 resolution: a repurposed multi-storey car park **in the neighbourhood core, working as a destination**. The ground floor is transformed into gastronomy, shops, and cultural and community space — permanent programme, not vendors ([[Gastronomy and Permanent Programme]]); upper floors open to housing, studios or event space; fleet functions operate at the perimeter and in the surrounding open space, mostly out of the building itself.

The L-Anchor answers the group's sharpest internal critique — "these are garages, not hubs" — by insisting that where an L-hub sits in the centre, it must behave as an anchor of public life, not a depot. Its unresolved implementation wrinkle: the web tool's placement algorithm excludes L candidates from the dense cores, so it cannot currently place L-Anchors where the concept wants them ([[L-Hub Anchor vs Depot]]).
