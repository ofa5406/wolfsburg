# Fleet Maintenance Station

The workshop layer of the L tier: inspection, cleaning, repair and sensor calibration for the 763-vehicle fleet — mandatory at every [[L-Hub]]. A shared fleet works its vehicles far harder than private cars ever work (that is the efficiency), so maintenance is continuous industrial activity, not an occasional garage visit; the system's reliability promise is manufactured here.

Locating maintenance inside repurposed car parks retains their industrial character honestly — ramps, decks and clear spans convert directly to service lanes and bays. The station also anchors the L-hub's employment story: the post-car transition converts driving jobs into fleet-care jobs, and six neighbourhood maintenance stations distribute that work across the city rather than exiling it to a periphery depot ([[Volkswagen]]'s own service culture being the local skills base).
