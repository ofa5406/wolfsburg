# Toolpalette Category — Optional Elements

The context-responsive half of the palette: sixteen elements applied where location, surrounding programme or street character warrants them — from the [[Heated Waiting Zone]] and [[Solar Canopy]] to the [[Childrens Play Element]], [[Game Table]], [[Micro-library Book Exchange]], [[Pop-up Market Slot]], [[Parcel Pickup Lockers]], [[Tool Equipment Lending]] and [[Memory History Marker]].

The optional set is what makes "modular element types, context-specific arrangement" real: near a school the hub grows a play element; near care facilities, an [[Adaptive Mobility Dock]]; at a factory gate, [[Luggage Storage]]; on a former car park, the memory marker that tells the transformation story in place. The optional elements are also the palette's social conscience — most of them serve the neighbourhood resident rather than the traveller.
