# Shared EV Bay

Parking and charging bay for shared electric cars, in [[Zone 1 — Vehicle Edge]] at **M and L hubs only**. The bay is station-based car sharing made physical: a fixed, visible, reliable address for the 175 shared EVs ([[Shared EV Car]]) that handle the journeys the lighter modes cannot — families, luggage, night trips, out-of-town destinations.

Restricting the bay to M and L is a deliberate exclusion: an S-hub with car bays would re-import car geometry into exactly the fine-grained street fabric the system is freeing. The bay's charging is integrated per palette rule, and its Bremen lineage matters rhetorically — the mobil.punkt station bay is the proven, German, two-decade-old precedent for this exact element ([[Bremen mobil.punkt]]).
