# Vendor Kiosk Slot

A defined position for a small vendor — coffee, local goods — at **M and L hubs**. The slot is serviced space, not a built shop: the hub provides position, power and footfall; the vendor provides the reason to come five minutes early. Commerce at this micro-scale is a public-life technology with deep roots — the kiosk has activated street corners since the Ottoman *köşk* became the European news-stand ([[History of the Kiosk]]).

The slot's economics are symbiotic: hub traffic makes the vendor viable, and the vendor makes the hub inhabited, watched and warm — a professional pair of [[Eyes on the Street]] for the price of a concession. At L-Anchors the logic scales up from slot to permanent gastronomy programme.
