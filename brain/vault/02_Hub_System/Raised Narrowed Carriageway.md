# Raised Narrowed Carriageway

The carriageway lifted to footpath level and/or narrowed through the hub, creating a traffic-calming moment that integrates the crossing into the hub — standard at M-hubs, the second [[Street Connectivity]] strategy. Vertical deflection is the most reliable speed control in the traffic-calming repertoire: geometry enforces what signs only request.

Raising the road to the pedestrian's level rather than ramping the pedestrian down to the road's reverses a century of kerb logic, and reads instantly: through the hub, the car is a guest on someone's floor — the [[Woonerf]] condition compressed to a hub-length. Combined with the stone surface and the narrowed width, the element lets each of the 68 hubs double as a calming device in the [[Five-Zone Model]]'s slow internal network.
