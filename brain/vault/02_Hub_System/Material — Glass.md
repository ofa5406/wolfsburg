# Material — Glass

The transparency material: shelter walls, the enclosed [[Heated Waiting Zone]], info-terminal faces, and the L-Anchor's opened ground floors. Glass performs a safety function disguised as an aesthetic one — a waiting space you can see into and out of keeps the [[Eyes on the Street]] contract intact after dark, which is why enclosed shelters at exposed sites are glazed rather than panelled, following the Zurich shelter precedent. In the Rhino layers, glass distinguishes the climate-controlled envelope elements from the open-frame family.
