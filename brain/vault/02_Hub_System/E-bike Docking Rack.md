# E-bike Docking Rack

Shared e-bike docking with integrated charging — the last-mile backbone of the whole network and a **mandatory element at every tier**, sitting in [[Zone 1 — Vehicle Edge]]. At the smallest S-hub the e-bike rack often *is* the mobility offer; at M and L it anchors the cycle end of the intermodal chain.

The rack's ubiquity is deliberate system design: 68 docking points across the city give the shared e-bike the same everywhere-availability that made the private bicycle indispensable, while integrated charging keeps the fleet's 131-strong e-cargo contingent and the standard e-bikes ready without a separate energy footprint. Charging is built into the furniture per palette rule ([[Charging Point]]), and directional lines in the stone field lead straight to it.
