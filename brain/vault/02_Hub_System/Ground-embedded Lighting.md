# Ground-embedded Lighting

Lighting set into the stone field surface so the hub stays visible and navigable after dark — mandatory at every tier. The embedded fixtures continue the [[Directional Lines]] into the night: the same paths that guide by day glow by night, keeping the [[Smooth Usage]] principle running around the clock.

Ground light flatters the hub's architecture — the field reads as a luminous carpet rather than a pool under a mast — but its real constituency is the night user: the shift worker crossing to a 4 a.m. micro-pod, the teenager waiting alone. Uniform low-level light with no dark pockets is a safety specification wearing an aesthetic disguise, and its pairing with the canopy's [[Ambient Responsive Lighting]] gives each hub two independent night layers.
