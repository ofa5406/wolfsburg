# S-Hub

**"The Moment" — 43 hubs · last-mile · walking radius 150–250 m.** The smallest tier: you arrive, grab an e-bike or wait briefly for a micro-pod, and leave. Compact but unmistakably a hub — never just a bike rack on the pavement. The ground field spans the full street section (10–15 m across, 15–20 m along), turning the street into a shared surface for the hub's length.

Mandatory equipment: [[E-bike Docking Rack]], [[Private Bike Parking]], [[Micro-Pod Zone]], [[Charging Point]], [[Hub Identity Marker]], [[Directional Lines]], [[Tactile Paving Strips]], [[Canopy Beacon]], at least one [[Bench]], and [[Ground-embedded Lighting]]. Standard additions include a tree, ambient lighting, community board and art marker. Placement is transit- and activity-derived — the [[Grasshopper S-Hub Placement]] algorithm grows the S network out of bus stops and footfall, making S the least parking-bound tier.
