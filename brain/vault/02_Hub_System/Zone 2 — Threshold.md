# Zone 2 — Threshold

The middle band of the [[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]]: the moment of orientation between motion and dwelling. Overhead stands the [[Canopy Beacon]]; at eye level the [[Hub Identity Marker]] names the hub, states its tier and maps the neighbouring hubs; at M and L tiers the [[Real-time Info Terminal]] shows live arrivals and vehicle availability. [[Directional Lines]] converge here before fanning out to the docks.

The threshold is where the network becomes legible as a system — the one place at every hub where you can answer "where am I, what is available, where next?" without asking anyone. It is deliberately the most standardised zone across all 68 hubs: identical information design in identical position builds the trust that lets a visitor use an unfamiliar hub as confidently as their home one.
