# Wildflower Meadow Strip

Low-maintenance seasonal planting for biodiversity, applied where manicured beds do not suit the context. The meadow strip is the palette's wilder register: instead of clipped municipal greenery it offers seasonal succession, pollinator habitat, and a maintenance regime of one or two cuts a year — cheaper than lawn and richer than it.

Ecologically the strips matter as network, not as patches: hubs strung along streets create pollinator stepping-stones through the urban fabric, a corridor function that mirrors the mobility network above it. Aesthetically the meadow signals a changed contract with the street — the reclaimed land is allowed to be alive rather than merely tidy, extending the [[Toolpalette Category — Environment]] agenda from climate engineering to habitat.
