# AV Staging Area

The L-tier's holding and dispatch ground for autonomous vehicles: where pods and buses wait between assignments, queue for the shift-wave surge, and stage overnight. Mandatory mobility infrastructure at every [[L-Hub]]. A demand-responsive fleet is never all in motion — the staging area is the buffer that absorbs the difference between fleet size and instantaneous demand.

Its sizing derives from the fleet mathematics: peak-hour dispatch for the [[VW Shift Wave]] requires vehicles pre-positioned, charged and sequenced, which is warehouse choreography more than parking. Concentrating staging at six L-hubs keeps the rest of the network clean — S- and M-hubs see vehicles only in motion or briefly docked, never stored, preserving the place-first character of the neighbourhood tiers.
