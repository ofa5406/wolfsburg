# Group Seating

The larger seating configuration for social groups, in [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]] at **M and L hubs** — arranged so people can face each other, not just the street. Where the single [[Bench]] serves the individual pause, group seating serves conversation: the school group waiting together, the neighbours who met at the kiosk, the card game that colonises a Sunday afternoon.

The element operationalises the sociable half of [[Catalyst Not Shelter]]. Seating geometry is behavioural design — rows produce waiting, angles produce talking — and the palette's distinction between the two seating types encodes that knowledge. Placed near the [[Game Table]] and [[Vendor Kiosk Slot]], group seating completes the small ecology that keeps an M-hub inhabited between transfers.
