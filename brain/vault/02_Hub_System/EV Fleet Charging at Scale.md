# EV Fleet Charging at Scale

Bulk charging infrastructure for the shared fleet — mandatory at every [[L-Hub]], and the reason the repurposed car parks are such convenient hosts: their electrical service, structure and vehicle circulation were built for exactly this footprint. Hundreds of vehicles cycling through charge windows demand grid connections, load management and thermal safety at industrial grade.

Charging strategy is fleet strategy: charge windows are scheduled into the anti-cyclical valleys of the demand curve (vehicles charge while the city sleeps or works, not during shift waves), and load management negotiates with the grid questions the future-projections research flags — aggregate demand, peak coincidence, storage buffering ([[Electric Mobility]]). The element is the energy heart of [[Hub as Infrastructure]], deliberately quarantined at six sites.
