# Ambient Responsive Lighting

Lighting integrated into the canopy that **brightens on approach and stays atmospheric at low-use hours** — standard at S-hubs, expected above. The responsiveness solves a genuine dilemma: a hub inside residential fabric must be reassuring to its users and unobtrusive to its neighbours, and a sensor-dimmed canopy is bright exactly when and where someone is present.

The element makes the hub legible at the city scale too: a constellation of gently glowing canopies marks the network across the night city, the [[Vertical Beacon]] principle in its after-dark form. For the three-shift rhythms of the [[VW Shift Wave]], responsive light is commuting infrastructure — dawn departures and midnight returns happen under it for half the year.
