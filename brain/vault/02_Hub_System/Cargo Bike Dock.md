# Cargo Bike Dock

Docking for cargo bikes, shared or private, in [[Zone 1 — Vehicle Edge]] — mandatory from the M tier upward. The cargo bike is the system's answer to the trips that keep households clinging to a car: the weekly shop, the flat-pack run, two children and their luggage. The fleet includes 131 e-cargo bikes ([[E-Cargo Bike]]), and the dock is their street address.

Where demand is high the dock extends into a [[Cargo Bike Lending Library]] with longer-term borrowing. The dock also carries a logistics role: local deliveries by cargo bike replace van traffic inside the filtered zones, so the same element serves the resident on Saturday and the courier on Monday.
