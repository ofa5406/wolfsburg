# Charging Point

Electric charging **integrated into docking furniture and structural elements — never freestanding posts.** Mandatory at every tier. The rule is aesthetic discipline with system consequences: 68 hubs bristling with bollard-style chargers would read as clutter, so energy delivery hides inside the e-bike rack, the EV bay kerb, the canopy column.

The element makes every hub a node of [[Electric Mobility]]'s energy layer: trickle charging for e-bikes and pods at S, car-rate charging at M, and the heavy fleet charging concentrated at L as the separate [[EV Fleet Charging at Scale]] element. Integration also future-proofs the palette — charging hardware evolves faster than street furniture, and hardware embedded in replaceable furniture modules can be swapped without rebuilding the hub.
