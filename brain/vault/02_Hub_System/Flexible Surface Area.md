# Flexible Surface Area

A clear, unobstructed paved area for temporary events, markets and gatherings — **M and L hubs**, where the ground field is generous enough to hold a void. Programmed emptiness is the hardest element to defend in a design review and the most valuable on a Saturday: it is where the pop-up market lands, the neighbourhood fest assembles, the school performance spills.

The element encodes [[Chrono-Urbanism]] at hub scale — one surface, many clocks: transfer space at rush hour, market at the weekend, play after school. Its emptiness must be defended in the plan drawings exactly like an occupied element, with dimensions and clearances, or accretion will fill it; hence its status as a named palette element rather than leftover space.
