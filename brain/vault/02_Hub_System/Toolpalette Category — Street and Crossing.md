# Toolpalette Category — Street and Crossing

The elements through which the hub annexes the street: the [[Hub-Integrated Crossing]] where the stone field continues across the carriageway, the [[Raised Narrowed Carriageway]] that lifts and slows traffic through the hub, and [[Both-Sides Distribution]] where the hub's elements occupy both pavements with the crossing as their hinge.

These three are simultaneously palette elements and the [[Street Connectivity]] strategies — the category exists because connecting both sides of the street is a required behaviour of every hub, not an occasional site condition. Together they make each hub a point where the pedestrian network takes priority over the vehicle network, embedding [[Traffic Calming]] into the mobility system itself rather than applying it as separate street furniture.
