# System Identity

The first design principle of the hub typology: the 68 hubs must form a **recognisable network** — a design family with consistent language that makes the system visible, navigable and tactilely coherent across the whole city. Every hub belongs unmistakably to the same system; every hub is also responsive to its immediate context. The formula: **modular element types, context-specific arrangement.**

Identity is carried by repetition of a few strong signals — the reddish [[Ground Field]], the [[Vertical Beacon]], the standardised [[Zone 2 — Threshold]] information design — rather than by logos. The precedent logic comes from transit branding: Bremen's mobil.punkt pillar or Vienna's WienMobil signage prove that a consistent mark turns scattered stations into one trusted service. The [[Kit-of-Parts]] method is the production machinery behind the principle.
