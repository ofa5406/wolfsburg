# Toolpalette Category — Typology Specific

The heavy infrastructure that exists only at the L tier: the [[AV Staging Area]], [[EV Fleet Charging at Scale]], [[Fleet Maintenance Station]], [[Full Fleet Representation]] and the [[Gastronomy and Permanent Programme]] that defines the L-Anchor's character. These are not street furniture — they are the back-of-house of the entire 763-vehicle fleet, concentrated where a repurposed car-park structure can absorb them.

Separating this category protects the rest of the palette from depot logic: an S- or M-hub never needs to host maintenance bays or bulk charging, so the neighbourhood tiers stay light and place-like while the six L-hubs carry the industrial load. The category is the palette's answer to the [[Hub as Infrastructure]] reading — real, necessary, and quarantined to where it belongs.
