# Both-Sides Distribution

The third [[Street Connectivity]] strategy: the hub distributes its elements across both sides of the street, with the crossing as the link between two halves — standard at M-hubs on wider sections. Rather than forcing every element onto one crowded pavement, the hub becomes a pair of complementary banks: docking on one side, dwelling on the other, stitched by the crossing.

Distribution suits streets too wide or too trafficked for a full shared surface, and corner sites where the hub naturally wraps. The strategy's success metric is that users perceive one hub, not two half-hubs — carried by the continuous ground-field language, sightlines between the halves, and directional lines that treat the crossing as an internal corridor rather than a boundary.
