# Five-Zone Model

The traffic architecture of the plan, locked as a design decision on June 11: central Wolfsburg divided into **five zones inspired by Groningen's 1977 Verkeerscirculatieplan**. Each zone is internally calm — one-way internal streets, priority for shared mobility, cycling and pedestrians — and through-traffic between zones is impossible: private vehicles must exit to the ring to enter another zone, while walking, cycling and the shared fleet cross zone boundaries freely.

This is [[Filtered Permeability]] executed at city-sector scale with zone entrances as the modal filters. Direct access is maintained for residents, deliveries and services, so the model restricts *routes*, not *people*. The zones also define the fleet's operational design domain: predictable, low-speed, filtered streets are exactly the environment where Level-4 autonomy works earliest ([[Vehicle Automation Levels]]). Its precedent lineage runs through [[Groningen Traffic Circulation Plan]] and [[Ghent Circulation Plan]].
