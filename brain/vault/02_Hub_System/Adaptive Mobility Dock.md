# Adaptive Mobility Dock

A dock for adaptive mobility equipment — accessible vehicles, mobility scooters, handcycles — applied near residential care, medical facilities, or wherever accessible-vehicle demand concentrates. The element answers the sharpest equity critique of shared mobility: standard fleets quietly assume able-bodied users, and a system that replaces the private car must replace it for the people who depended on it most.

Placement logic is data-driven care: the dock follows the geography of need rather than being token-scattered. Together with [[Tactile Paving Strips]], level boarding at the [[AV Drop-off and Pickup Zone]] and the non-smartphone channel of the [[Real-time Info Terminal]], it forms the palette's accessibility spine — the equipment half of a promise the equity risk register holds the project to ([[Equity of the Post-Car Transition]]).
