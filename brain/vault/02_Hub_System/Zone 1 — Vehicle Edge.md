# Zone 1 — Vehicle Edge

The first band of the [[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]], directly against the carriageway: where the machines live. The [[E-bike Docking Rack]], [[Cargo Bike Dock]], [[Micro-Pod Zone]], [[Shared EV Bay]] and [[AV Drop-off and Pickup Zone]] all sit here, with [[Charging Point]] infrastructure integrated into the docking furniture rather than standing free.

The zone's design problem is choreography at the kerb: vehicles of five different sizes and speeds arriving, waiting and leaving without conflicting with each other or with the pedestrians crossing the zone to reach them. Directional lines in the stone field lead users to each dock type, and clear dimensional allowances keep AV geometry honest. The vehicle edge is deliberately thin — machines occupy the minimum territory, because the hub's centre of gravity belongs to people.
