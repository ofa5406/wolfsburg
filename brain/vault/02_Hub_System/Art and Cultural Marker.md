# Art and Cultural Marker

A site-specific artwork integrated into the hub — **one per hub, designed with the site**, standard from the S tier. The one-per-hub rule solves the standardisation paradox: the system's identity depends on 68 hubs looking alike, but a neighbourhood's affection depends on its hub being *theirs*. The art marker is the sanctioned point of uniqueness inside the shared language.

Integration is the operative word — the artwork belongs to the hub's fabric (a relief in the stone field, a treatment of the canopy, a cast element in the seating) rather than being an object dropped beside it. Together with the [[Memory History Marker]] at transformation sites, the marker gives each hub a voice in the city's story, not just a function in its network.
