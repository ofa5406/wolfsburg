# Tool Equipment Lending

A small neighbourhood tool library at hubs near residential areas: the drill, the ladder, the pressure washer — objects owned by everyone and needed by each household four times a year. The element extends the system's core logic beyond vehicles: [[Access over Ownership]] applies to a hammer drill even more obviously than to a car, since the drill's idle rate is worse.

At the hub, tool lending inherits foot traffic, storage, and the trust infrastructure of the mobility account — one membership, many shared things. It deepens the hub's identity as the neighbourhood's sharing depot and pairs naturally with the [[Bike Repair Station]] and [[Cargo Bike Lending Library]] into a small commons cluster at the dwelling edge.
