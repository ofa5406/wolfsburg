# Material — Lighting

Light treated as building material: the ground-embedded fixtures in the stone field, the responsive luminaires in the canopy, the glow of the info terminal. Modelling lighting as its own material layer in the Rhino kit keeps the night hub a designed object rather than an afterthought — fixture positions travel with the element blocks, so every sample scene can be read by day and by night. The layer implements the [[Toolpalette Category — Lighting]] elements and the network's night identity of gently glowing beacons.
