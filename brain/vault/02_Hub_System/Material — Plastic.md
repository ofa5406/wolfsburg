# Material — Plastic

The palette's polymer register, used sparingly and deliberately: charging-hardware housings, locker doors, play-element surfaces, tactile studs, cable management. Plastic goes where impact resistance, colour-fastness, electrical insulation or moulded ergonomics beat the alternatives — and nowhere else, since the material's cheapness reads instantly and would undercut the system's public dignity. As a Rhino layer it isolates the replaceable, product-like components from the built fabric, matching their shorter maintenance cycles.
