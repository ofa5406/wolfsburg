# Material — Concrete

The palette's mass and plinth material: foundations, seat plinths, kerb transitions, and above all the retained frames of the six multi-storey car parks that become L-hubs. At the L tier, concrete is the material of [[Adaptive Reuse]] itself — the embodied-carbon argument for keeping the garages rests on their vast existing concrete structure, re-dressed rather than demolished. In the kit-of-parts, concrete elements anchor the composition where permanence and weight are the message.
