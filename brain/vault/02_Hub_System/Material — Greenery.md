# Material — Greenery

The living material layer: hub trees, planting beds, bioswale vegetation, wildflower strips and living walls. Treating greenery as a *material* in the kit — with its own Rhino layer alongside stone and steel — encodes the [[Catalyst Not Shelter]] doctrine structurally: plants are specified, budgeted and modelled like any other component, not sprinkled on at render time. The greenery layer is also the palette's time dimension, the only material that improves for fifty years while the rest depreciates.
