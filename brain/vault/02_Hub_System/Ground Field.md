# Ground Field

The hub's territory made material: a **distinctive field of reddish stone cube paving** that every hub occupies, regardless of tier. The field is rectangular, aligned with the street's direction, and scaled from context rather than fixed — S-hubs run roughly 10–15 × 15–20 m, M-hubs 18–25 × 25–35 m, L-hub outdoor areas 30–50 × 40–60 m. Crucially, the field **includes the road surface**: hubs are street-section redesigns, not sidewalk installations, and the stone crosses the carriageway.

The surface does system work. [[Directional Lines]] embedded in it navigate users toward each mobility element; [[Tactile Paving Strips]] mark every transition and boarding zone; [[Permeable Surface Zone]] sections handle stormwater; [[Ground-embedded Lighting]] keeps it legible at night. Its lineage is the [[Woonerf]] shared surface, and across 68 hubs the recurring red field is the network's strongest identity signal.
