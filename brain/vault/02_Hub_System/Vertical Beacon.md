# Vertical Beacon

The design principle that every hub must be **readable from fifty metres**: a canopy or structural element that signals the hub's presence at a distance, the way a metro sign or a clock tower works. Where the [[Ground Field]] marks the hub's territory for those already there, the beacon recruits users who are still a street away — the network advertising itself without a single billboard.

The principle is realised by the [[Canopy Beacon]] element at S and M tiers, and may merge with the [[Public Transport Shed]] into one unified structure where a bus route overlaps. The exception proves the rule: an [[L-Hub]] needs no beacon because the repurposed car-park building *is* the vertical signal. Together with the red field, the beacon is the second pillar of [[System Identity]].
