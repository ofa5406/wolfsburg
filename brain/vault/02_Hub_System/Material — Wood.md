# Material — Wood

The palette's warm register: bench slats, group-seating decks, canopy soffits, kiosk cladding. Wood is deployed where skin touches the hub — everything a person sits on, leans against or waits under — because its temperature, acoustics and ageing read as hospitable in exactly the places steel would read as institutional. In the Rhino kit it is a dedicated material layer, keeping the tactile elements visually and structurally distinct from the metal frame family they attach to ([[Material — Metal]]).
