# Toolpalette Category — Lighting

The smallest category with the longest hours: [[Ground-embedded Lighting]] set into the stone field, and [[Ambient Responsive Lighting]] integrated into the canopy — brightening on approach, settling to a low glow in quiet hours. Both are about night-time trust: a shared-mobility system that feels unsafe after dark loses exactly the users (women, night-shift workers, the elderly) whose confidence decides whether it is truly public.

Wolfsburg's three-shift factory clock makes this concrete — the [[VW Shift Wave]] includes departures and arrivals in full darkness for much of the year, so hub lighting is commuting infrastructure, not atmosphere. The responsive dimming is also a neighbour-friendliness move: hubs sit inside residential fabric and must not become floodlit islands.
