# Tree with Stone Grate

A tree planted within the stone field, its grate integrated flush with the paving — shade, identity and climate benefit in one element. Standard at S-hubs, mandatory (minimum one) from the M tier. The flush grate is the detail that matters: the tree belongs to the ground field's continuous surface rather than interrupting it, so the field stays barrier-free for wheels, canes and prams.

The tree is the palette's slowest element — planted at hub construction, mature decades later — which makes it the system's commitment device: a hub with a growing tree is a promise that the hub is permanent. Sixty-eight-plus hub trees also form a distributed canopy grid across the city, cooling precisely the paved places where people wait.
