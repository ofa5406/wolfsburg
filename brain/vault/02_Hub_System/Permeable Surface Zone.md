# Permeable Surface Zone

Sections of the ground field executed in permeable paving so stormwater infiltrates on the spot — mandatory from the M tier. The element edits the field's material recipe without breaking its visual unity: same reddish stone language, different joint and bedding, invisible to the eye and decisive for the water balance.

Its rhetorical weight exceeds its engineering: every square metre of parking asphalt was a sealed surface, and the hub that replaces it literally re-opens the ground. Multiplied across the network and the reclaimed streets, permeability is one of the quantifiable environmental dividends of the [[From Parking City to Hub City]] shift — measurable in runoff coefficients the way reclaimed land is measurable in square metres.
