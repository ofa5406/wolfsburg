# Real-time Info Terminal

The digital display at [[Zone 2 — Threshold]] of M and L hubs: live arrivals, **vehicle availability at this hub**, and the system map. The availability display is the M-tier's defining promise — "at an M-hub you can see what vehicles are available, live, before you decide" — turning the abstract MaaS platform into glass and pixels at eye height.

The terminal is where digital and physical wayfinding meet: the same data that drives the phone app appears in public, so the system remains usable without a smartphone — an equity requirement, not a redundancy. Sizing it to M and L only keeps S-hubs sign-free per the [[Smooth Usage]] principle; at an S-hub the offer is simple enough to see directly.
