# Toolpalette Category — Shelter and Comfort

The elements that make waiting and staying dignified: the [[Canopy Beacon]] overhead, [[Bench]] and [[Group Seating]], the [[Drinking Water Point]], and the [[Bike Repair Station]]. They concentrate in [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]], with the canopy marking [[Zone 2 — Threshold]].

Comfort is strategy, not amenity. Transfer research shows perceived waiting time shrinks dramatically when the wait is sheltered, seated and interesting; a hub that treats waiting with contempt loses its users back to the door-to-door car. Wolfsburg's climate raises the stakes — hence the optional [[Heated Waiting Zone]] as the category's cold-weather extension, following the Zurich precedent of enclosed, heated tram shelters ([[Zurich VBZ Tram Shelters]]). In the viewer legend this family renders in muted teal.
