# Childrens Play Element

A play element applied near schools, parks and family-residential areas — the feature that makes a hub a family destination. Its inclusion encodes a demographic strategy: parents are the household segment whose car dependence is hardest to break (school runs, shopping, the sheer logistics of small children), and a hub their children ask to visit converts a transfer point into a willing stop.

Play at the hub also normalises the system for the next generation — children who grow up playing beside micro-pods will never regard shared mobility as a sacrifice. Safety geometry governs placement: the play element belongs deep in [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]], buffered from the vehicle edge by the full width of the dwelling zone's planting and seating.
