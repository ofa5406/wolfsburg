# Material — Metal

The workhorse frame material: canopy structure, docking racks, repair-station fittings, locker banks, light masts. Metal carries the palette's loads and its precision — the elements that must survive weather, vandalism and twenty years of daily locking and docking are steel and aluminium at their core, often warmed with wood where hands land ([[Material — Wood]]). As a Rhino material layer it groups the structural skeleton of most mobility and shelter elements, from the [[Canopy Beacon]] down to the smallest rack.
