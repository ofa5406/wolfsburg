# Three-Zone Arrival Sequence

The spatial grammar of every hub: elements arrange across the ground field in three bands running from the carriageway inward. [[Zone 1 — Vehicle Edge]] holds docking, arrival and departure, EV bays and charging; [[Zone 2 — Threshold]] holds information, identity and wayfinding under the canopy; [[Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge]] holds seating, shelter, greenery and social elements against the building edge.

The sequence is a design principle, not a rigid template — real contexts compress, expand or reorder it, and corner hubs wrap it (Zone 1 facing both streets, Zone 3 in the inner corner). Its purpose is **smooth usage in both directions**: arriving by vehicle you decelerate through machine, information, and dwelling; arriving on foot you pass from calm through orientation to motion. The choreography replaces signage — the arrangement itself tells you where to go, which is the [[Smooth Usage]] principle at work.
