# L-Hub

**"The Neighbourhood Anchor" — 6 hubs · full fleet · walking radius 700–1000 m.** A different category, not a scaled-up M: the building or ensemble *is* the identity, so no canopy beacon is needed. All six sites are existing multi-storey car parks — the Parking City's own monuments recycled into the Hub City's anchors ([[Adaptive Reuse]]).

Mandatory mobility infrastructure: [[AV Staging Area]], [[EV Fleet Charging at Scale]], [[Fleet Maintenance Station]] and [[Full Fleet Representation]] — all five modes present. Two scenarios exist: **A**, building reuse (transformed ground floor, upper floors opened for housing or studios); **B**, pavilion clusters around a generous open space where no building exists. The June 17 rethink split the tier by role into the central [[L-Anchor]] and the edge [[L-Gateway]] — resolving the "anchor versus depot" tension documented in [[L-Hub Anchor vs Depot]]. Outdoor space carries the full S+M palette plus game tables, living walls, market slots and a [[Memory History Marker]].
