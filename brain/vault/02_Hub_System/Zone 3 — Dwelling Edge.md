# Zone 3 — Dwelling Edge

The innermost band of the [[Three-Zone Arrival Sequence]], against the building edge or open space: where the hub stops being infrastructure and becomes a place. [[Bench]] and [[Group Seating]], the [[Drinking Water Point]], the [[Community Information Board]], trees and the [[Planting Bed and Bioswale]], play and fitness elements, the [[Bike Repair Station]] at its edge — everything that rewards staying rather than passing.

The dwelling edge is the physical expression of the [[Catalyst Not Shelter]] principle: a transit shelter serves the wait, but a dwelling edge serves the neighbourhood, drawing people who are not travelling at all. Its quality is what Gehl-style observation would measure — optional and social activities happening at a mobility node — and it is the zone that most distinguishes the project's hubs from the fleet-depot reading of the same locations.
