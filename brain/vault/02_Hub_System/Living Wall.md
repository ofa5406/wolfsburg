# Living Wall

A vertical garden on L-hub facades — written specifically for the repurposed multi-storey car parks, whose vast blank walls are suddenly an asset: hectares of vertical surface available for planting. The living wall softens the garage's brutal elevations, insulates, cools, and hosts habitat, converting the building's most hostile feature into its most photogenic one.

Symbolism does heavy lifting here: greenery visibly colonising a parking structure is the [[From Parking City to Hub City]] thesis rendered as a single image, which is why the element recurs in the project's before/after visualisations. Maintenance realism belongs in the spec — irrigation, access, plant selection for north faces — since a dead living wall argues the opposite case with equal eloquence.
