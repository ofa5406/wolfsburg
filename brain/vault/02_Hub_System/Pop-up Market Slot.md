# Pop-up Market Slot

A serviced, regular vendor surface in L-hub open spaces: power, water and level ground for the weekly market, the seasonal stand, the food truck rotation. Where the [[Vendor Kiosk Slot]] hosts one permanent micro-business, the market slot hosts a rhythm — the Thursday vegetable stall that makes the hub a date in the neighbourhood's week.

Markets are the oldest hub programme in urban history; re-attaching them to mobility nodes restores an ancient pairing (people change vehicles where people trade) that the car city severed. Operationally the slot leans on the [[Flexible Surface Area]] and the L-hub's generous outdoor dimensions, and it gives the [[Gastronomy and Permanent Programme]] a street-level, low-rent counterpart.
