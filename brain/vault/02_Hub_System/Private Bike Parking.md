# Private Bike Parking

Secure parking for personally owned bicycles, mandatory at every tier and positioned near [[Zone 2 — Threshold]] where passive surveillance is strongest. Its presence in a shared-mobility palette is a point of principle: the system pools cars, but it has no quarrel with the privately owned bicycle — the one private vehicle whose storage footprint is trivial and whose use the project wants to multiply.

Practically, private bike parking is transfer infrastructure: the resident rides their own bike to the hub, docks it securely, and continues by pod or bus — the classic Dutch station pattern at neighbourhood scale. Security is the design variable that decides usage, hence the threshold-zone placement under lighting and eyes rather than at the hub's dark edge.
