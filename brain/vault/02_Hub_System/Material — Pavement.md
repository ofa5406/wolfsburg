# Material — Pavement

The signature material of the whole system: the reddish stone cube paving of the [[Ground Field]], plus its tactile and permeable variants. Pavement is the material that carries the network's identity — one recognisable red surface repeated 68 times across the city — and most of its wayfinding, since the [[Directional Lines]] and [[Tactile Paving Strips]] are worked into the stone rather than applied onto it. In the Rhino kit-of-parts, pavement is one of the material-based layers on which the 45 element blocks are organised.
