# Tactile Paving Strips

Raised tactile surface at **all zone transitions, boarding areas and crossings** — the accessibility standard, present throughout every hub at every tier. The strips give blind and low-vision users the same information the directional lines give sighted ones: where the zones change, where vehicles board, where the street begins.

Their universality is the point of principle: accessibility is specified as a property of the standard hub, not as a retrofit or a special variant. Combined with level boarding at the AV zone, the [[Adaptive Mobility Dock]] option near care facilities, and audio channels in the info terminal, the strips anchor the claim that a post-car system must be *more* accessible than the car city it replaces — for the third of the population that never could drive.
