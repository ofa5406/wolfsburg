# Park-and-Switch Interchange

The project's adaptation of classic park-and-ride for a hub city: private cars arriving from the region park once at an edge [[L-Gateway]], and their occupants *switch* to the shared system — an autonomous bus for the trunk leg, a micro-pod or e-bike for the last stretch. The name shift matters: "ride" implies boarding one transit line; "switch" implies entering a whole mobility system with one account and many modes.

The interchange resolves the tension between a car-light interior and a car-dependent hinterland without moralising at commuters. Design requirements follow from dignity at the seam: short, weather-protected transfer walks, guaranteed vehicle availability at peak inbound waves, luggage storage near the switch point, and pricing that makes the switch obviously cheaper than driving in. It is push-and-pull compressed into a single building.
