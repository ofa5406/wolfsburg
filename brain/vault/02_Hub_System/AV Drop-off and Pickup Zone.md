# AV Drop-off and Pickup Zone

The dedicated marked zone where autonomous shuttle pods and buses stop, with clear dimensional allowance for vehicle size — **M and L hubs only**, in [[Zone 1 — Vehicle Edge]]. This is the hub's interface with the fleet's bigger vehicles: the 55 shuttle pods and 33 autonomous buses that carry trunk and pooled flows.

The zone's geometry is unforgiving in a way street furniture is not: turning radii, door positions, level boarding and clearance must match the vehicles exactly, and the palette flags this dimensional honesty explicitly. Level boarding makes the zone the accessibility-critical element for wheelchair users and prams, and its position determines the walking lines that the whole three-zone arrangement then choreographs.
