# L-Gateway

The edge sub-type of the [[L-Hub]], the second half of the 2026-06-17 resolution: a multi-storey car park at the city edge working as **fleet depot plus interchange**. Here the operational reading of the hub is legitimate — bulk [[EV Fleet Charging at Scale]], maintenance, overnight staging — combined with the [[Park-and-Switch Interchange]]: inbound commuters and visitors leave their private cars at the gateway and continue on the shared system.

The L-Gateway makes the system honest about regional reality. Wolfsburg's commuter shed drives in from towns the hub network will never cover; rather than pretend those cars away, the gateway catches them at the perimeter, keeping the interior zones car-light without walling the city off. Open question tracked in the decision log: whether gateways count within the six L-hubs or form a separate fleet layer.
