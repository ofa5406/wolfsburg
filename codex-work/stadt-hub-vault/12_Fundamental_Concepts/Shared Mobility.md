# Shared Mobility

## Definition

A mobility model where vehicles are accessed as a service and used by many people across the day.

## Why It Matters Here

It is the operational mechanism that allows far fewer vehicles to replace many private cars.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not the same as public transport. It includes bikes, pods, shuttles, car-share EVs, and buses.
