# Five-Zone Model

## Definition

The Wolfsburg centre traffic structure inspired by Groningen, dividing the centre into access zones.

## Why It Matters Here

It gives the post-car proposal a circulation logic beyond isolated hub placement.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not a generic pedestrian zone. It is a filtered access model.
