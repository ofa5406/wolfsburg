# Parking City

## Definition

The existing condition in which land, streets, and buildings are organized around storing and moving private cars.

## Why It Matters Here

It is the problem condition the project wants to make visible.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not only surface parking lots. It includes garages, on-street parking, overwide roads, and car-first access logic.
