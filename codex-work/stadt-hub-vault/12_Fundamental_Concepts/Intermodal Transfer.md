# Intermodal Transfer

## Definition

The moment or place where a user changes from one mode to another.

## Why It Matters Here

Hubs must make transfers easy, legible, and comfortable.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not just proximity of vehicles. It includes waiting, wayfinding, accessibility, and safety.
