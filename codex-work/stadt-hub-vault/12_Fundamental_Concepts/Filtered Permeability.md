# Filtered Permeability

## Definition

A traffic strategy that prevents through-traffic while retaining local access.

## Why It Matters Here

It is the spatial behavior-change mechanism behind the 5-zone model.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not full car exclusion. It makes car routes less direct than alternatives.
