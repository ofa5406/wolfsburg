# Car-Dedicated Land

## Definition

Urban land used primarily for private car storage, circulation, access, and residual road capacity.

## Why It Matters Here

This is the spatial budget of the project. Reclaiming it creates the urban design payoff.

## Do Not Confuse With

Broader than parking. It includes overbuilt roads and car-oriented fragments that prevent public life.
