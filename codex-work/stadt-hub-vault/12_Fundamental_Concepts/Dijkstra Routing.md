# Dijkstra Routing

## Definition

A shortest-path algorithm used to route cycling connections through weighted street networks.

## Why It Matters Here

It gives the cycling network a reproducible routing method.

## Do Not Confuse With

It finds least-cost paths according to chosen weights; the weights are design assumptions.
