# Peak Hour Demand

## Definition

The maximum hourly load the system must handle, especially the 08:00 commuter peak.

## Why It Matters Here

Fleet sizing is governed by peak load more than by daily totals.

## Do Not Confuse With

Different from daily demand. A city can have manageable daily totals but fail at peaks.
