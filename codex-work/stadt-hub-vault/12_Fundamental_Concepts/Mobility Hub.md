# Mobility Hub

## Definition

A spatial node where shared modes, transfers, charging, wayfinding, and public-life functions meet.

## Why It Matters Here

It replaces parking as the project's organizing element.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not merely a garage or depot. The design concept protects the hub as a public place.
