# Hub as Place

## Definition

The idea that a hub must create public presence, identity, comfort, and social value.

## Why It Matters Here

It guards the project from becoming a hidden fleet logistics proposal.

## Do Not Confuse With

Different from hub as depot. A place is experienced by residents and visitors.
