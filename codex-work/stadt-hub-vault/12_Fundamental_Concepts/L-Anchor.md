# L-Anchor

## Definition

A central L-hub subtype that reuses a car-park building as a mixed public neighbourhood anchor.

## Why It Matters Here

It protects the spatial/public-life thesis of the large hub.

## Do Not Confuse With

Different from an edge fleet depot. It is central, programmatic, and civic.
