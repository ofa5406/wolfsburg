# Hub City

## Definition

The proposed condition in which shared access is organized through a network of mobility hubs rather than private parking.

## Why It Matters Here

It is the project's main transformation: Parking City -> Hub City.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not just replacing parking lots with stations. It requires public-life programming, network proof, and phased behavior change.
