# Public Life

## Definition

The social, civic, ecological, and everyday uses that can occupy land once it is not dominated by parked cars.

## Why It Matters Here

It keeps the project from becoming only a mobility optimization exercise.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not decorative streetscape. It is the actual urban reason to reorganize mobility.
