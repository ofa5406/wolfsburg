# Fleet

## Definition

The total set of shared vehicles that replaces private car trips in the proposal.

## Why It Matters Here

Fleet size drives capacity, hub footprint, charging needs, and proof credibility.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not the same as vehicles on street at one moment. It includes reserves, charging, and maintenance.
