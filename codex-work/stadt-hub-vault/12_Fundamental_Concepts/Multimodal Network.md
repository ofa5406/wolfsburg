# Multimodal Network

## Definition

A network where walking, cycling, buses, shuttles, pods, e-bikes, and shared EVs work together.

## Why It Matters Here

The project depends on combining modes, not on one perfect vehicle type.

## Do Not Confuse With

Different from mode stacking. The modes must connect spatially at hubs and operationally through the system.
