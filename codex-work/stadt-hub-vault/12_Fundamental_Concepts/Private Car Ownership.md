# Private Car Ownership

## Definition

The model in which mobility access is tied to individually owned cars that are idle most of the day.

## Why It Matters Here

The project attacks the land cost of ownership, not only the emissions of driving.

## Do Not Confuse With

Different from car use. A shared car can exist in the future system without recreating private storage demand.
