# OSM Data

## Definition

OpenStreetMap-derived geographic data used for roads, parking, transit, parks, cycling, and facilities.

## Why It Matters Here

It is the main spatial data source for the tool.

## Do Not Confuse With

OSM data is uneven and requires preprocessing; do not treat it as municipal certainty without checking.
