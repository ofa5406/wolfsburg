# Walking Catchment

## Definition

A human-scale access radius based on how far a user can reasonably walk to a hub.

## Why It Matters Here

It tests whether the network is usable in everyday life.

## Do Not Confuse With

Different from service radius used for fleet operations or large coverage graphics.
