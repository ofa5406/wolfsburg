# Reclaimed Land

## Definition

Space released from car storage or car-priority circulation and reassigned to public life, greenery, housing, or civic use.

## Why It Matters Here

It is the benefit side of the mobility argument.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not automatically green space. It requires design decisions about program, ownership, access, and maintenance.
