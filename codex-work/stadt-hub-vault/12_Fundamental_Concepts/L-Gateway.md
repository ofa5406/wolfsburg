# L-Gateway

## Definition

An edge L-hub subtype for fleet depot, maintenance, charging, and park-and-switch interchange.

## Why It Matters Here

It resolves the problem of where private regional car access meets the post-car center.

## Do Not Confuse With

Different from a central public anchor. Its public role is threshold and interchange.
