# Cycling Network

## Definition

The proposed or analyzed bike network connecting hubs, settlements, and destinations.

## Why It Matters Here

Cycling is a practical mode that supports the post-car shift before full autonomy is mature.

## Do Not Confuse With

Not just existing cycle paths. It includes missing protected segments flagged for intervention.
