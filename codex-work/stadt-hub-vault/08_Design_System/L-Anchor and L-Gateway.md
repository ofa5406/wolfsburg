# L-Anchor and L-Gateway

The June 17 resolution splits the L-hub idea into two public roles.

- L-Anchor: central reuse of former multi-storey car parks as mixed public anchors.
- L-Gateway: edge fleet depot and park-and-switch interchange for inbound commuters.

## Unresolved

Whether L-Gateways count toward the six public L-hubs remains an implementation question.
