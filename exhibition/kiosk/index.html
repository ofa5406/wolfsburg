04(Problems)

Ecological
  • constant CO₂ + NOₓ load from car traffic
  • large areas consumed by parking alone
  • fragmented green space, urban heat islands
Social
  • no car — no full access to city life
  • steep core–periphery divide
  • streets unsafe for children and the elderly
Urban
  • monofunctional zones force daily car trips
  • car infrastructure dominates public space
  • weak transit, no intermodal transfer points

05(Idea)

Accessed, not owned — one vehicle serves many people instead of sitting idle 23 hours a day.

No driver cost — frequent, on-demand service becomes viable even in the low-density periphery.

Charged at the hubs, centrally managed — zero tailpipe emissions in the city.
